When you submit www.lutzfinger.com to Google Search Console, choose the
"HTML tag" verification method and paste the content= value into
src/consts.ts → SITE.verification.google.

If GSC offers you a verification HTML file instead, drop it here as
public/google<hex>.html with the exact filename GSC gives you, then push.

For Bing Webmaster Tools: same pattern, but the meta tag is named
"msvalidate.01" and the field is SITE.verification.bing.
