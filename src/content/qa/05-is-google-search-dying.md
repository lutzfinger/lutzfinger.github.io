_What changes when LLMs become the search interface._

> Shoppers already use ChatGPT and Claude to decide what to buy, and I have shown how I [bought coffee without ever touching a browser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cycJJsQIHRg). So brands rush to add a chat box. They try to mimic ChatGPT, and they fail. We saw the same pattern in the early days of Google, when every site tried to build search as good as Google's and could not. I am guilty of this mistake myself.

— [Three Mistakes Brands Make In The Age Of AI-Driven E-Commerce](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2026/05/23/three-mistakes-brands-make-in-the-age-of-ai-driven-e-commerce/)

> ** The interface is becoming a conversation. Shoppers expect to be understood, not handed a menu to navigate. Everyone uses ChatGPT, Claude and other LLMs to discuss what they want to buy. I showed in this video how I [bought coffee without ever touching a browser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cycJJsQIHRg). **The mistake we see.** Brands try to mimic ChatGPT. They try to be like it. Do not. You will fail. We saw this in the early days of Google, when everyone tried to offer search as good as Google's on their own websites, and yes, failed.

— [The State Of E-Commerce: Every Layer Of Discovery Changed At Once](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/state-e-commerce-finetuned-ai-every-brand-lutz-finger-gs5bf/)
