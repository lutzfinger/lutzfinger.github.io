_Lutz's take on OpenAI economics and moats._

> Should you fear ChatGPT will disappear? No, quite the opposite. ChatGPT has rapidly evolved into a prominent consumer-centric service. No other application has grown at such a pace. It's here to stay. Microsoft sees value in OpenAI as a tool to compete with Google and will likely continue to fund server costs. While bankruptcy might be off the table, the underlying business model of OpenAI is concerning. Selling Large Language models is not creating a business moat.

— [OpenAI Isn't Going Bankrupt, But It Has A Business Model Problem](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2023/08/18/is-openai-going-bankrupt-no-but-ai-models-dont-create-moats/)

> is more for Microsoft than for OpenAI. Regulatory Protection. Regulation can create a moat. Sam Altman has been on tour with policymakers advocating for more oversight and control. Additionally, OpenAI now allows blocking their scraping mechanism with a "NoAI" tag. Both efforts would create a moat: (1) regulators would allow OpenAI as a tool but be more restrictive to newcomers; (2) newcomers could no longer scrape data with a "NoAI" tag. While models do not have a moat, OpenAI is not doomed.

— [How OpenAI Can Secure Its Investments - A Playbook That Needs Microsoft](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2023/08/18/the-potential-moat-of-openaihow-openai-can-secure-its-investments/)
