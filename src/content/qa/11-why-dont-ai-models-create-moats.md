_The structural reasons foundation models commoditize._

> Early in the LLM hype, I wrote that a model moat does not exist for OpenAI — such a moat also did not exist in previous Machine Learning waves. No Model Moat: transformer models are math. Math is not protected. It's a neural network — neural networks are essentially stacked linear regressions within activation functions. One cannot protect them easily. No Data Moat: general knowledge is widely available.

— [DeepSeek - New Economic Rules And Regulatory Challenge](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2025/01/29/deepseeknew-economic-rules-and-regulatory-challenge/)

> forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2023/08/18/is-openai-going-bankrupt-no-but-ai-models-dont-create-moats/). Workflows do. That is the whole reason [SaaS is not dead](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2026/04/24/saaspocalypse-is-deadthe-future-of-saas-is-saas/), despite a year of obituaries. SaaS can build the new AI-driven workflows on top of its user data and customer surfaces. But workflows are brittle. One new prompt, one model update, and the whole chain quietly breaks.
