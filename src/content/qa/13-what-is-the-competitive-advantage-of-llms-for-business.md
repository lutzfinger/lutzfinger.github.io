_How enterprises can build durable AI advantage._

> Three Takeaways.  Do large language models create a moat? AI-driven businesses have a combination of either a model moat, a data moat, or the brand moat. The new LLMs, like OpenAI's model, can give an advantage in all areas, but it is by no means a given. Since OpenAI launched ChatGPT, large language models have entered the fantasies of founders worldwide. Every day about a dozen new startups announce their AI-enabled feature. The industry is hype, and many VCs are driven by FOMO. (1) MODEL MOAT.

> But the company understands what it needs to do. Here is its playbook: (1) Invest in a better User Experience focused on LLMs; (2) Create a feedback loop that improves their LLMs over time; (3) Integrate into many workflows to improve utilization; (4) Enterprise (private) data is king — allow BYOD (bring your own data); (5) Ask the regulator to protect you. Many of those potential moats depend on Microsoft. User Experience. ChatGPT has a moat — 100+ million consumers go to ChatGPT and no longer go to Google with their questions.
