_Vendor risk and architectural diversification._

> Stargate's $500B investment — message to the competitors — meant to intimidate potential challengers with overwhelming financial backing. AI is extremely dangerous; only a select few should be allowed to develop it — OpenAI's storyline to use safety concerns as justification for a regulatory setup that creates barriers to entry and gives them more monopolistic power. From now on, models should respect IP rights — OpenAI ignored IP…
