_Lutz's view on AI regulation._

> But regulation doesn't happen in a vacuum. The EU has regulated that gender should not be used in AI models — even though this may lead to less effective risk distribution and higher overall costs. As a society, we may be willing to pay that price, but what if other societies are not? Data is the lifeblood of AI.

— [Emmanuel Macron On AI: We Are Regulating Ourselves Out Of The Market](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2024/10/22/emmanuel-macron-is-right-about-ai-over-regulatingi-have-no-doubt/)

> To regulate AI, we need to understand that AI isn't a character from a movie. AI is a tool made to help us, and regulations should focus on how we choose to use it — not on the technology or the model. I analyze any AI product by: 1. control, 2. data, and 3. transparency. Control AI.

— [Biden's AI Plan And Why Central Control Might Not Work](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2024/10/26/bidens-ai-plan--why-central-control-might-not-work/)
