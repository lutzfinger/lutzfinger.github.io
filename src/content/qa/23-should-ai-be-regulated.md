_Lutz's view on AI regulation._

> If we continue with our usual agenda over the next 2 to 3 years, we will be out of the market. I have no doubt!" I'm building a startup in the AI space, advising a venture capital firm on AI, and have been teaching AI for over 10 years. And I agree with Macron. Citizens, Governments, and Businesses. Europe talks about regulating AI but seems to miss that there is a balance between fostering innovation and upholding societal values. There are three key levers when talking about regulation. Control Of AI Systems.

> The main idea aims to have control over AI. That's a good start, but the White House seems to think AI can be controlled, like nuclear weapons. But it's not that simple. This plan focuses on "national security" (mentioned 68 times) more than "responsible" use (18 times) or transparency (2 times). Understanding AI: It's Not An Entity. To regulate AI, we need to understand that AI isn't a character from a movie. AI is a tool made to help us, and regulations should focus on how we choose to use it — not on the technology or the model.
