> How To Spot Social Media Bots - They Are Often Lonely How To Spot Social Media Bots - They Are Often Lonely Created on 2015-02-25 16:28 Published on 2015-02-25 16:28 Social media bots have become an increasingly challenging issue. They can trick you into buying stuff or even influence your opinion (read more about the trouble they can cause here). But one way to spot – and stop – bots is by using their own friends. Who wants to have friends who are just there to ‘sell you stuff’. Correct! No one!
