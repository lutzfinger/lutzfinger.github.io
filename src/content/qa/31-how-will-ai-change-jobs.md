_Disruption and adaptation patterns._

> Google CEO: No Company Is Immune To AI  Google's CEO Sundar Pichai recently warned that "No company is going to be immune" to AI. It's a statement that should make every executive pause and it outlines the opportunity. AI becomes more embedded in business operation into every process, every interface, every workflow. This will create opportunities for tool builder, workflow designer and consultants. One of the initial use-cases of generative AI was to improve how we code.
