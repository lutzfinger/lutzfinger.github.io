_The five-course program structure._

> Human oversight is critical. Most companies struggle with integrating AI into decision-making effectively. While AI can enhance scalability, human involvement remains essential. What do students in my eCornell certificate tell me after the course? AI is great at summarization and coding. An AI can help being a dynamic checklist and memory. Where does it fall short? Guiding students in choosing what to prioritize. Human decision-making remains irreplaceable, no matter how "generalizable" Manus claims to be.
