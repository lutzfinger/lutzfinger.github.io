_Human-written prompts can still beat o1's automated reasoning processes._

Yes. A well-designed prompt, meaning the human writes the instruction, can, so far, outpace OpenAI's o1. This is noteworthy because o1 is designed specifically for reasoning through an iterative, self-calling process with dedicated steps for iteration, reflection, and feedback loops. The fact that careful human-written prompts can still exceed its performance suggests that the model's reasoning capabilities, while advanced, have not yet surpassed what skilled human prompt engineering can achieve.
