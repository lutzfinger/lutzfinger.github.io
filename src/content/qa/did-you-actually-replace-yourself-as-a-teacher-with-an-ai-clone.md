_A teaching experiment reveals AI's limits in education._

I built one of the first online certificates, Designing and Building AI Solutions, where I replaced myself with an AI clone. The clone is public. It's now a no-code course where students can imagine business ideas and have the AI clone write the necessary code. We have a 24/7 teaching assistant. But did this mean I replaced myself as a teacher? Not even close. AI can summarize information, but it can't predict future trends. Teaching is more than just delivering knowledge. It's about understanding and evaluating AI trends to find the best ways to use AI for business.
