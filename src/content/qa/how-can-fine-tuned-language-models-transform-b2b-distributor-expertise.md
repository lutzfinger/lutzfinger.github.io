_Decades of technical knowledge, now instantly accessible at scale._

A fine-tuned, domain-specific LLM can capture and deliver what once lived only in the minds of seasoned technical staff. The accumulated knowledge behind decades of "call Bob in tech support" scenarios, including spec sheets, material tolerances, and compatibility nuances, can now be made instantly accessible at zero marginal cost. This scales expertise in a way that was previously impossible, turning institutional knowledge into a programmable, always-on asset.
