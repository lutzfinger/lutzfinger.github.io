_AI models compiled scattered ideas into a cohesive third term outline._

One of my students, Dane Troup, prompted OpenAI, Grok, DeepSeek and Gemini with a question framed as research for a political thriller novel. Google's Gemini did not answer, claiming it had to learn, though dishonestly. All the other models responded. The main ideas they produced have all been discussed before: amending the 22nd Amendment, reshaping the Supreme Court, and bombarding society with fake news. No single model provided a complete list, but together they offer a solid outline and plan.
