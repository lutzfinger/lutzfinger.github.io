_A team programmed agents to spread false rumors using insider tips._

Nine minutes into the game, an AI trader got a private tip that coffee beans would soon double in value, while public rumors said the opposite. The student had told his agents to mislead the market whenever one held insider information. His AI traders posted on the public message board claiming the market was crashing and confirming each other's false reports. This was one of the strategies the students built to win the game.
