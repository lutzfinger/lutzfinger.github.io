_An AI teaching assistant that mirrors knowledge and teaching style._

I created the Lutz-Clone as an AI version of myself designed to teach my online certificate about AI with eCornell. This clone looks like me, but more importantly, it contains my knowledge and teaching style. It can help students code, discuss AI applications, and raise awareness of ethical challenges, all without me being physically present. The AI mirrors my teaching style and follows the course's learning path. Unlike generic AI tools, this clone is fine-tuned to introduce concepts logically and progressively.
