_A virtual instructor keeps course content fresh and current._

I launched a public certificate program on product and AI at Cornell's Johnson School. The course is about 100 hours long. To keep the content up to date, I replaced myself with a bot, a virtual copy of myself. This allows the course material to stay current without requiring me to manually update everything. It's an example of how AI can maintain educational content in a scalable way while I focus on other work, including running my Gen AI platform for e-commerce.
