_EU grants rights against automated decisions; US relies on external investigations._

In the US, algorithms are not allowed to discriminate against race, but without transparency it is hard to surface those biases. It often takes external investigations like ProPublica's study showing Asian families were charged higher prices or the ACLU's analysis revealing Amazon's AI incorrectly identified 28 Congress members as criminals. In the EU, citizens have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated means. However, it would be more powerful if mandatory registers were established making this information publicly accessible.
