_Quality metrics for healthcare ML._

> Knowing quality is therefore of utmost importance. Three broad measurement areas in today's healthcare: (1) Metrics about quality of care — related to treatment effectiveness and patient satisfaction. (2) Metrics about need and utilization of care — gauging health requirements of a population and how often one uses what type of service. (3) Metrics about value-based healthcare — they measure disease prevention rather than disease…
