_You remain accountable for your AI agents, like a CEO for employees._

If people write an email and that email is wrong, who is accountable for it? Should I say it was my AI agent? Not quite. It was me who trained and set up and focused an AI agent. We talk about the new role that all of us become managers or CEOs of many agents. If that description is true, then the accountability lies in you, the CEO. When something happens at a traditional corporate, the CEO might lose their job if somebody of their employees did not do the right thing, because ultimately they are responsible.
