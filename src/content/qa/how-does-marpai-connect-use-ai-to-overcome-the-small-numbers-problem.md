_Pooling clients and predictive AI create scale for small employers._

Marpai Connect addresses the small numbers challenge by pooling all self-insured clients and anticipating an overall reduction in aggregate. We use various approaches from simple time series modeling to advanced deep learning models like Neural Prophet to predict risk based on longitudinal claims records. This pooling strategy allows us to achieve the statistical power that individual small employers lack, while still delivering personalized care through AI matchmaking that pairs each user with the most suitable value-based care partner.
