_Summarizing mountains of patient data to surface what truly matters._

In healthcare, patients often overwhelm doctors with massive amounts of data. For example, a patient with atrial fibrillation might bring five years of sleep data from their smartwatch, or a cancer patient could arrive with a 20-pound stack of medical records. What's needed is an AI that can summarize and highlight the key points. Multimodal AI handles diverse data sources like text, images, audio, video, and time-series data simultaneously, offering a richer, more comprehensive understanding to help doctors focus on what matters most.
