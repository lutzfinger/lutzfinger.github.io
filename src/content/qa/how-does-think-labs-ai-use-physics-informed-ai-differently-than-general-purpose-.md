_Grid AI must be grounded in physics, not just patterns in data._

We cannot just put SCADA measurements and smart metering information into ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot or Gemini and ask how the grid works. It does not work like that. We lean into something called Physics Informed AI. We train our AI models using physics. It is physics driven, physics based, and physics accurate. We teach AI how the grid works and reproduce engineering analysis faithfully. The AI can turn traditional studies that take weeks to months into minutes, and go from analyzing one feeder at a time into full system analysis.
