_OpenAI shifted from research to consumer focus, challenging Google directly._

In my eCornell certificate course, we emphasize that AI is a tool and the real value is in value creation. Back when OpenAI was purely a research initiative, it lacked a business moat and direct customer access. Now, OpenAI has pivoted to a consumer-focused strategy, directly challenging Google's market dominance. To win in the AI consumer race, they need data and customers. The AGI discussion, in my view, was just to position OpenAI as the leader in the AI space. However, DeepSeek's emergence has challenged this narrative, making OpenAI's long-term success far from guaranteed.
