_Coding is no longer the bottleneck. Business insight is._

LLMs can complete sentences, so they can complete code. Technology should not be a barrier anymore. When I started as a product manager in Silicon Valley, the ratio was one PM to ten engineers. Now it is one to one. Why? Because the main language for coding is English. The bottleneck is not coding anymore. It is business acumen. It is all the people who make METRO happen. Software delivery is up for grabs. We can do autonomous coding. The important part is having the controls at the end to ensure engineering excellence.

— [Schnitzel is AI](https://www.lutzfinger.com/transcript/2026-10-01-schnitzel-is-ai/) · _METRO AI Summit 2026_
