_AI search delivers up to 500% higher conversion than keyword search._

I did research comparing traditional keyword search with a more explanatory AI approach. When someone searches for a car like a Honda Odyssey, instead of just showing a list, the AI provides context about the car, options, and nearby dealerships with pricing. The conversion rate from search to buy can be five times higher in some cases. That's a 500% increase. This happens because the chatbot gives personalized recommendations based on knowing the user's full background and context, making the information land much more effectively than traditional search results.
