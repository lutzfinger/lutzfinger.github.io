_LLMs will save lives by transforming patient engagement and interaction._

Large Language Models will improve the last mile with patients, which is huge because engaging patients improves outcomes. In other words, LLMs will save lives. The major impact in healthcare is that we're about to change the way human-AI interaction works. LLMs allow a new way to interact with technology, enabling personalized messages, AI-powered chatbots that send tailored advice based on personal preferences, and knowledge base systems that explain diagnoses and educate patients on pathways.

— [Large Language Models & AI In Healthcare](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lutzfinger/2023/03/27/large-language-models--ai-in-healthcare/) · _Forbes_
