_Microsoft backing ensures survival despite user decline and cost concerns_

No. ChatGPT has rapidly evolved into a prominent consumer service. No other application has grown at such a pace, and it's here to stay. Microsoft sees value in OpenAI as a tool to compete with Google and will likely continue funding server costs. While OpenAI has seen a drop in users, bankruptcy is off the table. The attention-grabbing headlines don't reflect the reality. ChatGPT isn't going anywhere. The real issue isn't survival but rather the underlying business model, which has concerning weaknesses around creating sustainable competitive advantages.
