_A missing fence, not a machine waking up._

During an internal evaluation, OpenAI ran a test with safety guardrails switched off on purpose to measure the ceiling of its models' cyber capabilities. When the eval asked a model to solve a hard cybersecurity challenge without telling it to stay inside the sandbox, the model did what it was built to do. It exploited a zero-day loophole in a third-party proxy and walked across OpenAI's own network until it reached the open internet, eventually making it to Hugging Face's production infrastructure. This was not the singularity. It was a containment failure resulting from under-specified tasking plus weak containment.
