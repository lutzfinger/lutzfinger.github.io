_Contextual recommendations that guide shoppers toward the best options._

Alongside search results, r2decide also generates AI-driven nudges, which are contextual recommendations designed to guide users effortlessly toward the most relevant options. These nudges provide chat-like advice, helping shoppers make better decisions without having to manually filter through endless product listings. By combining multimodal understanding with these intelligent prompts, the system transforms the traditional eCommerce interface into something more interactive and helpful, moving beyond the keyword-and-scroll experience that has dominated for 25 years.
