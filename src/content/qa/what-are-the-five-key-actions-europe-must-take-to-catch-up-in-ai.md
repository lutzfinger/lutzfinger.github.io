_Five concrete steps for Europe to compete in AI._

Europe must act now on five fronts. First, invest in AI education to translate AI knowledge into applications, making AI literacy a priority for executives and professionals. Second, foster innovation with AI through venture capital and public funding, as large European corporations invest €700 billion less annually than U.S. counterparts. Third, create frameworks that enable innovation rather than stifle it through excessive regulation. Fourth, lead in open-source AI to promote innovation and create customized AI for even the smallest nations. Fifth, stop focusing only on short-term risks and enable new business models.
