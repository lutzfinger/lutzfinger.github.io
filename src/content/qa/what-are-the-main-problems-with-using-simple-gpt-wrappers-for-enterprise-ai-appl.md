_Why basic ChatGPT integrations fail in business settings._

GPT wrappers have several critical shortcomings that prevent them from working well in enterprise settings. They lack system integration, so they cannot check calendars or access other tools. They have no context about user preferences or past interactions. Security is missing, which means they could accidentally reveal private details. They lack guardrails to handle controversial questions appropriately. They offer no user control over outputs. And they suffer from hallucinations, where the AI sometimes makes things up. These issues make simple wrappers unsuitable for real enterprise deployment.
