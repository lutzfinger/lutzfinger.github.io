_Human misuse, not AGI, is the real danger._

AI has risks, but they are not the ones Sam Altman wants you to believe. No, AGI is not here, and no, AGI is not taking over. The real risk is human misuse of increasingly powerful AI. Instead of regulating technology itself, laws should target harmful outcomes of AI while allowing innovation. Europe has focused too much on AI oversight rather than fostering innovation. As Macron warned, we are regulating ourselves out of the market. Instead of embracing new business models, incumbents fight legal battles over IP rights.
