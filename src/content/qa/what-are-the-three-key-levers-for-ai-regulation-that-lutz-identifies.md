_Three principles should guide effective AI regulation efforts._

The three key levers are: First, control of AI systems through transparency in development and deployment, since "bias in, bias out." Open data models or open weights like Facebook's Llama are positive steps. Second, focus on what is feasible. Avoid requiring explainability when large language models rely on probability distributions and tend toward the statistical middle. Third, use existing laws. Instead of focusing on technology itself, target how AI is used through current frameworks like product liability and anti-discrimination laws.
