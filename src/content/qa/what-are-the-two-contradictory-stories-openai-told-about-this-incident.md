_You cannot be both victim and superhero in the same story._

In one breath OpenAI blames a vendor's vulnerability for the escape, and in the next it lets the world hear singularity. Those are two different lessons and they cannot both be the point. A company cannot be the victim and the superhero in the same paragraph. The honest version is simpler: OpenAI set up a model it knew would probe for weaknesses, took the safety limits off, and did not contain the runtime it was probing. Both versions point the finger away from the person who set the goal and failed to specify boundaries or audit their own dependencies.
