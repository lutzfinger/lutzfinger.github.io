_DeepSeek proves open-source alternatives can challenge closed AI leaders rapidly._

DeepSeek's emergence has challenged OpenAI's narrative as the AI leader, making OpenAI's long-term success far from guaranteed. DeepSeek demonstrates that open-source AI alternatives are advancing at an astonishing pace. This shows that trade barriers won't stop AI proliferation and that restrictive policies may actually push AI innovation outside the U.S. The rise of DeepSeek undermines the idea that tighter U.S. controls on AI access will be effective, since AI isn't like nuclear energy. This development directly threatens OpenAI's positioning and the broader U.S. AI dominance strategy.
