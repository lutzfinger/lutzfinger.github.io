_Public data is not a moat. Customer integration is._

Weather, local events, shelf life, and seasonality are available to everyone. Even past orders are not exclusive because I can estimate schnitzel consumption using satellite photos of parking lots to track foot traffic. The real competitive advantage comes from forward integration, getting closer to the customer. If METRO can access a customer's security camera feed, for example, you know what they are using, how often, which competitors they use, and when to deliver. That reduces their working capital, makes them more effective, and makes you stickier. Competitors and OpenAI do not have that data.

— [Schnitzel is AI](https://www.lutzfinger.com/transcript/2026-10-01-schnitzel-is-ai/) · _METRO AI Summit 2026_
