_Models are commodities. Custom harnesses and thinking create the edge._

Soon every company will execute its strategy through agents. The models will be the same for everyone. The harness, and the thinking behind it, will not. AI is a tool everyone has. Who wins still depends on who uses it best. Across every client I have worked with, no strategy came off the shelf with a model. Each one had to be built. The strategy has to fit your team, your product and your competition.
