_Business needs remain constant, workflows must evolve._

Our business hasn't changed. We still have needs, we still travel, we still consume, we still build. The only question is how can I do this business more effectively? Yes, there will be people who use ChatGPT to write an email, but the winners will be those who either build the platforms or rearrange the workflows in order to be effective. The focus is on the workflow, the surrounding, the setup. How do we empower you in your environment, not can you use it?
