_The partnership may be ending as AI economics shift fundamentally._

This bid could indicate that Microsoft and OpenAI's partnership is nearing its end. I've long suspected Microsoft might pivot away from OpenAI. Model commoditization and the trend toward more cost-effective inference suggest Microsoft's long-term AI focus will shift, similar to Amazon's, toward AI applications rather than frontier development. Perhaps this bid is a sign that Microsoft is looking to offload OpenAI. The bid itself could signal an issue with the Microsoft-OpenAI partnership, even though Sam Altman quickly rejected Musk's $97.4 billion offer.
