_Three dimensions reveal how AI products really work._

I analyze any AI product by three factors: control, data, and transparency. Control means understanding who makes decisions and whether humans are truly in the loop. Data looks at who owns and has access to the information that powers AI. Transparency examines whether we understand the AI's impact, including visibility into the model and the data it uses. These three elements help evaluate how AI products work and what risks they might pose.
