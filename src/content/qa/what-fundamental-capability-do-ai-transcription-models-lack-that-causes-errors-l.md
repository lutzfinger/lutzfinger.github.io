_AI generates likely sequences but cannot verify facts or cross-check reality._

AI transcription models don't verify facts. They don't pause and think, 'Merkel is no longer Chancellor, so that must be wrong.' They don't access live knowledge graphs or cross-check reality. They just generate the most likely sequence of words based on historical data. That's why AI feels brilliant in some moments and brain-dead in others. It reflects the averages of the past, not necessarily the truth of the present. The crucial gap is that these models cannot apply logic or verify their outputs against current reality.
