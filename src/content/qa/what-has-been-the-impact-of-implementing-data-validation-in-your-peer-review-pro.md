_Instructors now focus time on critical reviews, elevating course quality._

Employing data validation and data quality assurance has been a game-changer for the class. As instructors, we can now concentrate on the most critical reviews, ultimately elevating the course's quality. Instead of reviewing every piece of feedback, we focus our time on re-evaluating feedback from students who fit problematic personas. Our toolset is coded in Python and can be adapted for various courses using FeedbackFruits, making this approach scalable beyond just our Designing Data Products course.
