_The harness turns models into agents executing your unique strategy._

When agents execute, strategy runs through the harness you build around them: the data, tools, permissions, decision rules and checks that turn a model into an agent acting for you. Generic harnesses exist. The value lies in building yours. Designing a harness means designing how your company can win. The strategy has to fit your team, your product and your competition, which means mapping how your company actually works before you hand it to agents.
