_A two-year paid fellowship helping PhDs turn research into companies._

Runway is an entrepreneurship fellowship at Cornell Tech designed to help PhDs commercialize their research. The program accepts five fellows per year and provides a two-year paid fellowship specifically to commercialize technology that you ideally developed during graduate school. It's a structured way to transition academic research into viable commercial products, giving you financial support and time to build your startup while maintaining ties to Cornell Tech's ecosystem and resources.
