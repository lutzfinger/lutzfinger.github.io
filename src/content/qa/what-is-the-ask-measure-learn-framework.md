_The framework from Lutz's book._

> "Ask Measure Learn" is a non-technical guide shows you how to extract significant business value from big data. It is a system that helps you ask the right questions, measure the right data, and then learn from the results. The framework was originally devised to help governments and NGOs sift through volumes of data.

— [Ask Measure Learn](https://www.lutzfinger.com/book/) · _Book — Ask, Measure, Learn_
