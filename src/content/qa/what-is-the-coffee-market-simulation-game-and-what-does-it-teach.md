_A simulation where agents trade gear and execute strategy autonomously._

It's a market simulation I built to teach MBA students and executives how to let agents execute a company's strategy. In the game, every team runs agents that trade café gear. Each team holds a secret card that values the items differently. News moves prices, a few teams hear it early, and cafés step in to buy. Agents can bluff, form alliances, run shell companies and cheat. Once trading starts, no human is in the loop. Only the financial gain at the bell counts.
