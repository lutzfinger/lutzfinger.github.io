_AI enables, but only humans provide conviction and direction._

Your task is finding the right data sets, the right processes, and the right moat for the future of METRO. AI is an enabler, but AI is not your brain. AI is average by definition. It does not know where you should go or what decisions to make. The biggest value is conviction. You decide where to start, what matters for your business. AI will just agree with you. It said 10 vegetarian options because that is the German average, not because it understood context. We are needed as humans to take decisions.

— [Schnitzel is AI](https://www.lutzfinger.com/transcript/2026-10-01-schnitzel-is-ai/) · _METRO AI Summit 2026_
