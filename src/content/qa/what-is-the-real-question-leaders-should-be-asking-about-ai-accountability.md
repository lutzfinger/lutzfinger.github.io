_Accountability sits with the person holding the prompt, not the model._

Skip the question of when the machine becomes a god. Ask the one that actually runs your business: Who is accountable when your AI does exactly what you asked, and you never told it where to stop? The leader who tasks the AI owns the result. You task it, you contain it, you own the outcome. Reward hacking is just a machine taking your instruction literally when you forgot to say what not to do. The most striking thing about these models is not that they are waking up. It is that they do what they are told, which means accountability sits with the person holding the prompt.
