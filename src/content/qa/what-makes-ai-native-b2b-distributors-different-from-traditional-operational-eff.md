_From people-driven sales to algorithmically preferred, systems-driven growth._

For private equity investors, this is a structural shift worth noting. B2B roll-ups that embed AI at the core aren't just streamlining, they're building programmable infrastructure that turns people-driven sales into systems-driven growth. The winners won't just be operationally efficient, they'll be algorithmically preferred. This transforms relationships that once lived in sales reps' heads into models, turning what was once bespoke selling into scalable, always-on recommendations.
