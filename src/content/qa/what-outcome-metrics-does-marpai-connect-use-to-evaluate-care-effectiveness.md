_Specific metrics track adherence, improvement, and patient participation._

My team at Marpai designed outcome metrics with our value-based care partners to evaluate the effectiveness of care. These include medication adherence, functional health improvement, flu vaccination rate, and patient engagement. We also built financial alignment into the platform, where providers should only receive payment if they effectively help reduce costs and improve health on average. For example, NuvoAir committed to a total cost of care reduction of 35% based on claims analysis with their virtual-first care model.
