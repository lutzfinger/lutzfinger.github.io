_Nearly half of healthcare spending could be avoided with better care._

At Marpai Inc., we discovered that 23% of all healthcare costs are linked to reversible diseases. An additional 18% could remain stable with better management. That's a staggering 42% of essentially avoidable costs. This finding highlights the enormous opportunity to reduce healthcare spending by focusing on prevention and better disease management rather than just treating illness.
