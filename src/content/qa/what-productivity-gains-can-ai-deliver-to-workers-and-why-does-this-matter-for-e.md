_AI boosts effectiveness by 34% for certain tasks._

A recent study by Erik Brynjolfsson showed that AI can increase worker effectiveness by up to 34% for novice and low-skilled tasks. AI is a tool that scales productivity and supercharges humans. In my online AI courses, students no longer need to code manually. Instead, they use AI to amplify their productivity. Now imagine a world where the U.S. and China boost productivity by 34% while Europe remains stagnant. AI will not replace professionals like lawyers, doctors, or teachers, but those who use AI will outcompete those who don't. The same applies to nations.
