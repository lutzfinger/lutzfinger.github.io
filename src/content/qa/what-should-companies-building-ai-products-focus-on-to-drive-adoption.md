_Winning AI strategy prioritizes obvious value over safety messaging_

You don't need to convince people that AI is safe. You need to make the benefit feel immediate and concrete. The companies that will win are not the ones that make AI sound least scary. They're the ones that make the value proposition so obvious that the abstract worry doesn't win the tradeoff. Understanding this is the most important thing for anyone building AI products or strategies. Focus on making convenience and value clear, not on reducing fear.
