_Legal shields and lower compliance burdens are critical for AI startups._

Europe needs several regulatory reforms. First, imagine a European equivalent of Section 230 for AI, a legal shield letting startups experiment without fear of lawsuits. Without it, regulation-heavy environments scare away founders. Second, lower compliance burdens. GDPR was a privacy milestone but became a speed bump for innovation. My own AI startup, r2decide, worked with a German e-commerce brand, but every advisor warned me to avoid launching in Europe due to compliance burdens. Even Apple delayed Siri upgrades in the EU due to regulatory friction. Europe must stop trying to retrofit old frameworks to this new paradigm.
