_Mortality metrics can discourage doctors from treating complex cases._

As Michael Howell reminded me, the real challenges are in finding the metrics that matter and preventing unintended consequences with any given metric. Take a simple metric on mortality for surgery: patients should not die during surgery. But what if the patient's situation was complex to start with? There are cases where a patient did not receive care and died because giving him appropriate care could have resulted in a worse quality score for the doctor.
