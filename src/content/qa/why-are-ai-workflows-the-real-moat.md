_How redesigning work around AI beats model upgrades._

> SaaS can build the new AI-driven workflows on top of its user data and customer surfaces. But workflows are brittle. One new prompt, one model update, and the whole chain quietly breaks. I watch this every time I get students [coding agents in my Cornell workshops](https://ecornell.cornell.edu/courses/artificial-intelligence/building-enterprise-ready-ai-agents-from-prototypes-to-production/).
