_Visibility depends on what AI models learned during training._

If your brand isn't present in AI answers, you're effectively invisible. The reason is training data. Models surface answers based on the content they've been exposed to. For example, when someone asks ChatGPT about hiking jackets, it might recommend Outdoor Research, Arc'teryx, and Patagonia, but brands like Mammut, Columbia, or The North Face are absent. That's because companies like Patagonia and REI have done a better job supplying structured, high-quality information to AI systems. If you haven't taught the models who you are, you won't appear in their recommendations.
