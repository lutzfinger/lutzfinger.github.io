_Conversations replace quizzes when AI can pass every test._

Traditional quizzes are becoming obsolete. Standardized tests like the Medical College Admission Test can now easily be solved by AI. Since 2023, ChatGPT has consistently passed every single one of my multiple-choice questions on machine learning and real-world AI applications. Instead, we should shift from quizzes to conversations. In a world where knowledge is instantly accessible, education should focus on critical thinking and dialogue. I now use AI to discuss outcomes with students, encouraging them to evaluate, question, and apply their knowledge.
