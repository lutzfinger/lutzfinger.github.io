_Simple products worked, but complex specs exposed a critical gap._

Amazon's B2B distribution worked fine for simple products like paper towels, but the moment specs get tricky, its model breaks down. Most industrial products live or die on context: temperature thresholds, compliance codes, compatibility quirks. Amazon lacked this contextual ingredient. That's precisely what large-language models excel at. Transformer models can embed context, turning the messy details of industrial distribution into valuable assets for an industry still playing catch-up on technology.
