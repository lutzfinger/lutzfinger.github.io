_Google shifted from blocking to providing third term strategies with code updates._

Google initially restricted itself. When we tested in February, Gemini did not respond. Google has previously adopted a heavy-handed approach toward user freedom and free speech, tinkering with prompts to avoid showing images of the Founding Fathers. I say initially because Google has since updated its algorithm and now provides full insights. This demonstrates the power of these models: with a line of code, they can change what we see. Microsoft similarly changed course silently after I analyzed its AI incorrectly identifying Angela Merkel.
