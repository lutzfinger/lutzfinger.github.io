_The two fatal flaws in most 2024 AI implementations._

Many companies rushed to try generative AI in 2024, and some even used chained models. But most projects failed to scale for two main reasons. First, they were test projects not integrated into system infrastructure, meaning they existed in isolation rather than connecting to existing business systems. Second, they were missing guardrails and quality controls necessary for production deployment. Without proper integration and controls, these pilot projects could not move beyond experimentation to deliver real business value at scale.
