_Two tech giants admit the same problem in one week._

Sam Altman admitted AI adoption is slower than he thought because we haven't reached the "iPhone moment" where technology stops being a tool and simply becomes the way we work. He blamed economic inertia, saying humans are too change resistant. This admission came around the same time Meta quietly abandoned its plan to restructure its workforce around AI agents, with both industry leaders acknowledging the same challenge: deployment is harder than the pitch decks say.
