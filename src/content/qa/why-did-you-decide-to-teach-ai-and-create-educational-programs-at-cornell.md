_Understanding AI enables ethical deployment and smart business decisions._

I've been teaching at Cornell for over 10 years because AI is impacting all of us, everywhere. We saw deepfakes of Taylor Swift and Russia using AI to meddle in U.S. elections. My aim is to explain AI to everyone because only if we understand it can we deploy it ethically and use it in ways that make money for businesses. I wrote a book and started these activities alongside my real job because we need to understand AI to use it responsibly and effectively.

— [AI, Clones, Deep Fakes, and Neural Networks](https://www.lutzfinger.com/transcript/2024-09-12-ai-clones-deep-fakes-and-neural-networks/) · _The Keynote on AI_
