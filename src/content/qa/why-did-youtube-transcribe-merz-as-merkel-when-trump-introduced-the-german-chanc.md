_AI leans toward historical patterns, not current reality._

YouTube's AI transcription system made this mistake because for 16 years, 'German Chancellor' almost always meant Angela Merkel in the training data. When the model heard 'Merz,' which is acoustically close but far less common, it leaned toward the familiar, high-probability continuation: 'Merkel.' The model plays the odds based on historical patterns. It's like predictive text suggesting 'Year' after 'Happy New' instead of 'Birthday.' The AI isn't wrong in a statistical sense, it's just out of sync with the real-world moment.
