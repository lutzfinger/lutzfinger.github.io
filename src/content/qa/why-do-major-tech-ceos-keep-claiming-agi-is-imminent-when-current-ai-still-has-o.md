_Market pressures shape how companies talk about AGI timelines publicly_

There is tension between AGI timelines and current product reality. While Altman, Musk, and Hassabis suggest AGI is near or already here, anyone who codes knows current systems still fail frequently. At Google I/O, Demis Hassabis mentioned AGI by 2029 and being at the foothills of singularity, but this was buried at the end. Most of the keynote focused on incremental AI features in products. This makes sense because publicly listed companies need to highlight quarterly profits. Saying AGI comes in 2029 might cause investors to dump stock and wait. So companies focus on near-term utility while their research arms pursue AGI separately. The timeline itself has become more plausible to many, though skeptics remain.
