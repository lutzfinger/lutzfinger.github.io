_Strategy design matters as much as model strength in competition._

Models are average by default, and in my crowdsourced experiments, how the students designed their strategy mattered just as much. In an unbalanced market, a small model with low latency won. No single AI behavior fits every moment. Speed wins when news resets prices. Information wins when you act on it first. The best teams also read the rules more closely than anyone else. The winners were not the teams with the biggest model. They thought first, then told their agents what to do.
