_The gap between AI anxiety and behavior reflects human nature_

The gap between stated anxiety and actual behavior is not hypocrisy. It's just how humans work. We accept risks all the time when the benefit feels immediate and the risk feels diffuse. People worry about job displacement, privacy, hallucinations, and dependency. But the cost of non-adoption feels more concrete than the cost of risk. If you don't use AI and your competitor does, you lose. If you don't use AI and your colleague does, you fall behind. The fear is real, but the friction of not using it feels worse.
