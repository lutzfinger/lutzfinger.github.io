_Search poised to dominate 2025 as game-changing AI solution._

I believe if you're placing bets on a single technology to dominate the conversation in 2025, bet on search. By merging large language models, retrieval-augmented data, and intuitive user interfaces, search is poised to be the game-changing AI solution for both individual users and global enterprises. Technology has transformed the way we search for and interact with information, and if past patterns hold true, this is search's moment.
