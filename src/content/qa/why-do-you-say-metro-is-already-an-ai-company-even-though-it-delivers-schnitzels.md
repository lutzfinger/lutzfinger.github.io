_Delivering millions of schnitzels requires AI at every step._

METRO delivers about 20 million schnitzels a year, and that happens because of AI. The company uses predictive algorithms and forecasting to figure out which produce goes where, when, and at what time. This is machine learning, regression algorithms, old-school AI, not just chatbots. AI looks at tiny clues like past orders, seasonality, weather, and local events to forecast demand. So when I say schnitzel is AI, I mean METRO is using AI throughout its operations. OpenAI delivers zero schnitzels. You are the AI company.
