_AI optimizes for probability, not brave or emotionally resonant breakthroughs._

AI, by design, is average. It's spectacular at pattern recognition and synthesis, but it produces the most statistically likely output, not the most surprising, brave, or emotionally resonant. Imagine asking an AI in the early 2000s to design a music player. An AI would have designed a better CD player, not reinvented music distribution like the iPod did. AI fundamentally cannot make the kind of judgment calls that lead to breakthrough innovation because it optimizes for what's most probable, not what's transformative.
