_Underinvestment in training and punishing failure hold Europe back from AI._

Europe is not behind because it lacks brains. It is behind because it underinvests in training and adoption. In San Francisco, self-driving cars are a tourist attraction. In Europe, they're theoretical. Beyond training gaps, Europe doesn't lack risk-takers but penalizes them. In the U.S., failure is a badge of honor. In Europe, it's a career ender. We need policies like bankruptcy reform that give entrepreneurs a second chance. Europe has missed past digital revolutions including internet, cloud, mobile and social. Without ending the stigma of failure and accelerating adoption, infrastructure investment alone won't close the gap.
