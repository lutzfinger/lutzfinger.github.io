_Redesigning workflows while keeping business goals intact requires human expertise._

AI will change how work is done by embedding into every process, interface, and workflow. But our core business goals like operations, logistics, and finance won't change. AI will supercharge those workflows, and someone has to redesign them while keeping business goals intact. That someone is an MBA or consultant with new tools. Every organization will need to rethink how work gets done, which creates enormous opportunity for McKinsey, BCG, Accenture, and MBAs trained in AI-powered processes to answer how AI can support business objectives, reduce cost, and reshape workflows.
