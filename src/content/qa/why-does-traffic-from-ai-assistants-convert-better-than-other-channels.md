_AI recommendations convert nine times better through personalized trust._

Studies show that traffic originating from LLMs converts up to nine times better than other channels. Why? Because recommendations feel personal, contextual, and trustworthy. One example stands out: a consumer uploaded their blood results into ChatGPT and immediately purchased €200 worth of vitamins with no browsing, no comparison, pure trust. AI-driven traffic is still small in volume, but its impact is outsized. The personalized, context-aware nature of AI recommendations creates a level of trust that traditional search channels can't match.
