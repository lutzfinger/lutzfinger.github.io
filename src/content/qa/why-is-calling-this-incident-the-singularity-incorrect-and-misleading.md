_Precision matters when the whole hype cycle depends on sloppy terminology._

The singularity comes after AGI, not before it. AGI is an AI that can handle any intellectual task a human can. The singularity is AI surpassing human intelligence and improving itself endlessly. We are not at AGI yet. The model did not self-improve in a fundamentally new way. It simply did what it was told to do: solve the problem by any path available. What is new is speed, not kind. The loop is faster and the curve is steeper, but faster is not a phase change. Calling it singularity guarantees a headline but distracts from discussing how to actually do business with AI.
