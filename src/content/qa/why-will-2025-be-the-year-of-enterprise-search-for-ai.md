_Enterprise search becomes critical as AI agents need company data_

AI needs data to be effective. What we need are tools that can read and summarize company documents, tailored for enterprise use. 2025 will be the year of search, especially enterprise search. Companies that have access to this kind of data, like Microsoft, Amazon, Google, and Salesforce, are major players in the race to supply AI agents. Access to enterprise data is one of the two critical things companies need to become the main supplier of AI agents for tomorrow's workforce.
