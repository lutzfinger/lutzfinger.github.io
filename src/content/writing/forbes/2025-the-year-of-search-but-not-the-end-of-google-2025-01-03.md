# 2025: The Year Of Search But Not The End Of Google

*[Image: 2025: New Search Technology Will Put Google Under Pressure]*

It might sound like a throwback to the 1990s, but technology has transformed the way we search for and interact with information. If past patterns hold true, 2025 will be the year of search. Will it be the end of Google? No, but the fight is on.

A Snapshot of Internet History. Back in the 1990s, people said "All the information is out there at the internet... we just need to find it." That was the time of search engines — AltaVista, Yahoo! Search, Google, Lycos, WebCrawler, Excite. Even Disney tried its hand with Go (1998).

New Technology — New Search: Large Language Models Build AI Search. Large language models are trained on vast amounts of text. The underlying innovation is the attention mechanism. Not every word is important — models learn to extract the key ideas for a given context. This ability and their ability to complete the next best word makes them excellent at summarization.

Bye Bye, Google? Unsurprisingly, every major tech company — and many startups — are now racing to build or enhance AI-driven search. Google was late to the game, but its generative search experience is coming back quite strong. Microsoft has boosted Bing by integrating ChatGPT. Meta is developing its own search. OpenAI went from being a research organization to becoming a full-on consumer-focused search company. Perplexity focused on citation-based answers — users receive concise overviews with source links, which boosts credibility and user trust. Many startups are also coming to AI-based search.

What Do They Need to Win the AI Search War? Like in the 1990s, it all boils down to users. If a search tool can grab enough attention, keep people engaged, and build loyalty, it has a shot at success. Companies need to grasp the concept of the "long tail" in search. Human queries are inherently complex, which is why keyword-based search initially struggled. Understanding context while providing the right level of information is a challenging task. Google has a significant advantage with its years of experience and vast amounts of data. Don't count Google out just yet. The comeback it made with its generative search experience was impressive.

2025 — the Year of Search. If you're placing bets on a single technology to dominate the conversation in 2025, bet on search. By merging LLMs, retrieval-augmented data, and intuitive user interfaces, search is poised to be the game-changing AI solution for both individual users and global enterprises.
