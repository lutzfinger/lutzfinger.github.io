# AI Agents In 2025: What Enterprise Leaders Need To Know

*[Image: AI Agents for the Enterprise will be the focus of 2025]*

"2025 will be the year of the AI agent." Back in 2023, I talked about chaining AI models in my podcast. By 2024, many companies rushed to try generative AI, with some using chained models. But many projects failed to scale — they were test projects not integrated into system infrastructure, or missing guardrails and quality controls.

Why GPT Wrappers Are Not Enterprise AI. The simplest way to build an email-answering tool is with a GPT wrapper: connect AI to a small interface, get a ChatGPT API key, write code to take an email as input, add a prompt telling the AI what to do, and display the response. Even in this simple example, several key challenges emerge: No System Integration (can't check a calendar), No Context (doesn't know user preferences), No Security (could reveal private details), No Guardrails (can't handle controversial questions), No User Control, and Hallucinations (AI sometimes makes things up).

The Workflow of AI Agents: More Than Generative AI. AI models can be connected or "chained" to build workflows where the output of one model becomes the input for the next. Think of tools like Zapier or IFTTT, but powered by AI. Instead of fixed steps, the process is dynamic. For our email tool: Input arrives → System analyzes email and figures out steps needed → System checks calendar, looks up past emails, predicts likelihood of attending → Creates three draft replies (accept/decline/ask for more details) → Presents options to user. This chained AI model approach overcomes many earlier issues. System Integration checks calendars. Context Search (RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation) retrieves relevant past interactions. Traditional AI predicts likelihood of attending. User Design offers multiple options for control.

2025 - AI Agents for the Enterprise. 2025 will be the year of AI agents. AI will simplify, enhance, or automate workflows across industries. Instead of one "killer app," we'll see smaller workflows in areas like customer care, legal support, and sales. A practical framework for enterprise AI: Define the business objective → Collect and clean the data → Develop the agentic workflow → Test with users → Create a feedback loop. Most importantly, the focus must remain on value creation.
