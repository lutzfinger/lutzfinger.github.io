# Chris Hemsworth Super Bowl Ad Shows Why We'll Use AI Even If We Fear It

*[Image: Chris Hemsworth at European Gala Screening for Amazon's CRIME 101, London January 28 2026. (Photo by Kate Green/Getty Images for Amazon MGM Studios and Sony Pictures Entertainment)]*

Amazon's Super Bowl commercial for Alexa+ tells the most honest story about AI agency and AI adoption: even if we are worried about losing agency we still will use AI. In the ad, Chris Hemsworth imagines that Alexa+ could aim to kill him. The garage door, the pool cover, the fireplace all could be used to eliminate him. Yet he still uses it. Why? Convenience beats fear.

## The Alexa+ Super Bowl Ad

That's the actual insight of the ad: we know AI is risky — in abstract, theoretical ways — but we also know it makes our lives easier, and that frictionless convenience tends to win.

## AI Adoption Despite Fear

This pattern shows up consistently in the data. People express concern about AI in surveys. They worry about job displacement, privacy, hallucinations, dependency. And then they use it anyway.

The gap between stated anxiety and actual behavior is not hypocrisy. It's just how humans work. We accept risks all the time when the benefit feels immediate and the risk feels diffuse.

## Why Convenience Beats Fear

The answer is that the cost of non-adoption feels more concrete than the cost of risk. If you don't use AI and your competitor does, you lose. If you don't use AI and your colleague does, you fall behind.

The fear is real. But the alternative — the friction of not using it — feels worse.

## What This Means For AI Strategy

For anyone building AI products or strategies, this is the most important thing to understand. You don't need to convince people that AI is safe. You need to make the benefit feel immediate and concrete.

The companies that will win are not the ones that make AI sound least scary. They're the ones that make the value proposition so obvious that the abstract worry doesn't win the tradeoff.
