# Large Language Models & AI In Healthcare

*[Image: Large language models being integrated into health care workflows — Lutz Finger & Midjourney]*

Over the past few weeks, we have seen an ever-increasing number of companies that integrate generative artificial intelligence (AI) into their products. ChatGPT and other Large Language models are being added to features from Notion, Salesforce, Shopify, Quizlet, and others. What is the impact on healthcare? It will improve our last mile with our patients. This is huge as Engaging patients improves outcomes. Or said differently, Large Language Models (LLM) will save lives.

Large language Models have existed for several years, with their development and popularity growing rapidly since the mid-2010s. All of those models are so-called transformers, initially introduced in 2017. There were a few very successful early adopters — for example ultimate.ai, a company that automates every aspect of customer support. ChatGPT4 and others have dominated our public discussion lately. Other well-known models are GPT, BERT, Chinchilla, Gopher, Bloom, MT-NLG, PaLM, LaMDA.

Large Language Models (LLMs) are different from "traditional" Machine Learning models. Traditional AI is used to recommend your next movie at Netflix or determine whether some mail is spam. In healthcare, traditional models predicted disease deterioration or helped detect cancer. LLMs can be used for those tasks formerly done by more traditional models — and those foundation models might have better predictive accuracy and work with multimodal data. But in healthcare the major impact will be that we are about to change the way human-AI interaction works. LLMs allow a new way to interact with technology.

In my day job as President of Product and Technology at Marpai Inc., our core aim is to help self-insured companies not only administer their claims but also help members find the best provider for their needs. The digital health startup landscape is exploding — in 2021 alone we saw $29.1B of capital being invested by Venture Capitalists, and many are adding LLMs to their products. They are using AI technology to create personalized messages to help patients — AI-powered chatbots from Value-Based Care providers that send tailored advice based on a user's personal preferences, and knowledge base systems that explain a potential diagnosis and educate patients on pathways.

Healthcare providers are increasingly utilizing the immense potential of Foundation Models to promote patient engagement and adherence. By combining AI technology with traditional methods such as care management programs or wellness initiatives, healthcare providers have an enhanced opportunity for achieving better outcomes at lower costs — all while providing personalized, human-centric care. It's an exciting time to find the best AI-driven approaches for our healthcare system.
