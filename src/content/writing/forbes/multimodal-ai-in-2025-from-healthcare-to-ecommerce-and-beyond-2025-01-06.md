# Multimodal AI In 2025: From Healthcare To eCommerce And Beyond

*[Image: Multimodal AI Use Cases — Lutz Finger]*

Multimodality is set to redefine how enterprises leverage AI in 2025. Imagine an AI that understands not just text but also images, audio, and other sensor data. Humans are naturally multimodal. In healthcare, patients overwhelm doctors with data — a patient with atrial fibrillation showing up with five years of sleep data from their smartwatch, or a cancer patient arriving with a 20-pound stack of medical records. What's needed is an AI that can summarize and highlight the key points.

How Does Multimodality AI Work? Multimodal AI is designed to handle diverse data sources — text, images, audio, video, and even time-series data — at the same time. By combining these inputs, multimodal AI offers a richer, more comprehensive understanding. It is more of a discovery tool — different data modalities are stored by the AI, and once a new data point is input, the AI finds topics that are close. For example, inputting sleep data from a smartwatch alongside information about atrial fibrillation episodes might find indications of sleep apnea. This is the scaled-up version of what Amazon popularized: "people who shopped for this item also bought this item." In this case: "People with this type of sleep pattern have also been diagnosed with AFIB."

Multimodal Explained: Encoders, Fusion and Decoders. A multimodal AI system consists of three main components. Encoders convert raw data (text, images, sound, log files) into vectors stored in a latent space. Encoders can process virtually anything — images, text, sound, videos, log files, IoT sensor information, time series. Fusion Mechanism: when working with multiple types of data, we need to fuse the information to find what's most relevant. Decoders deliver information from the latent space to something we understand — for example, finding an image of a "house."

Transforming eCommerce with Multimodality. Amazon's interface hasn't changed much in 25 years — you type a keyword, scroll through results, and hope to find what you need. Multimodality can transform this experience by letting you describe a product, upload a photo, or provide context. At r2decide, we're using multimodality to merge Search, Browse, and Chat into one seamless flow. In an online jewelry store, a user searching for "green" would — in the past — only see green jewelry if the word "green" appeared in the product text. Since r2decide's AI also encodes images into a shared latent space, it finds "green" across all modalities. Users can also search for broader contexts like "wedding," "red dress," or "gothic." This capability even extends to brand names like "Swarovski," surfacing relevant items even if the shop doesn't officially carry Swarovski products.

AI-Generated Nudges to Give Chat-Like Advice. Alongside search results, r2decide also generates AI-driven nudges — contextual recommendations designed to guide users effortlessly toward the most relevant options.

Multimodality in 2025: Infinite Possibilities for Enterprises. Multimodality is transforming industries, from healthcare to eCommerce. Startups like TC Labs use multimodal AI to streamline engineering workflows, while Toyota uses it for interactive, personalized customer assistance. 2025 will be the year multimodal AI transforms how enterprises work.
