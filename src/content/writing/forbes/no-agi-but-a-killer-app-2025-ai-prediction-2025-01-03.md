# No AGI But A 'Killer App' — 2025 AI Prediction

*[Image: We have heard a lot about AGI but it won't happen in 2025]*

No Artificial General Intelligence and the Singularity in 2025. Both have been hot topics in AI, sparking both fear and excitement. Sam Altman predicted AGI would arrive in 2025, and Elon Musk 2026. Both are more about hype than reality. In 2025, there will be no AGI but large language models will find their "killer app."

What are AGI and the Singularity? Artificial General Intelligence: an advanced AI that can think, learn, and solve problems across a wide range of tasks, just like a human. The Singularity: the idea of AI surpassing human intelligence, improving itself endlessly, and causing massive, unpredictable changes in society. My prediction: we won't see any of this in 2025.

Sentence Completion is Not Intelligence or AGI. Generative AI like GPT models can hold human-like conversations but is limited to spotting and repeating patterns. ChatGPT works by predicting the most statistically probable next word or token based on training data. Input: "Life is like a box of…" → Prediction: "chocolates." This isn't real understanding — it's just pattern matching. Generative AI doesn't "consider" other options. It might seem intelligent because it can give polished responses, but it's no more self-aware than a chess computer that doesn't care if it loses.

OpenAI's o1: Isn't That the First Step for AGI? No. OpenAI's O1, released in 2024, does not directly answer a given question. Instead, it creates a plan to determine the best way to answer it, then critiques its response, improves it, and continues refining. This chained output is truly impressive. But is this intelligence? No — it's a fixed framework and not dynamic. The more critique cycles you allow the model, the more facets it can address, but this comes at the cost of increased time.

The Barriers to Get to AGI. Humans can think fast and instinctively (System 1) or slowly and logically (System 2). AI only works through patterns, missing this balance. AI struggles with context and often misses important details humans pick up naturally. Current AI builds outputs on previous ones (autoregressive models), so mistakes can snowball. Many researchers believe much of this might be fixable — by 2050? 2030? But not this year.

What Will Happen in 2025? In 2025, we'll see more narrow AI solutions integrated into chains similar to OpenAI's o1 approach. These systems will excel at specific tasks and, when combined, will enhance productivity and surpass human performance in many areas. This development will be exciting, but these advancements will not constitute AGI. And Sam's Claim of AGI? It's mostly marketing. Big, bold claims grab attention. The promise of AGI positions OpenAI as the leader in the AI space — but the real story in 2025 will be the killer application of large language models.
