# Three Trends Of Transformation: How AI & Value-Based Care (VBC) Will Drive Transformation Of Self-Insured Healthcare

*[Image: AI Foundational models can predict risks further into the future compared to traditional machine learning]*

Healthcare is on the brink of a seismic shift, and AI is its catalyst. Three converging trends are creating a perfect opportunity for change: (1) Foundational Models' growing ability to predict risks further into the future compared to traditional machine learning, (2) the rise of the self-insured healthcare industry motivated to address these risks, and (3) a surge in Value-Based Care (VBC) providers focused on risk reduction. These three forces promise to redefine healthcare fundamentally.

Trend 1: Alignment in Healthcare and the rise of the Self-Insured Industry. Research found that a significant proportion (⅓ to ¼) of hospital admissions in the US are avoidable. At Google, my team and I created predictions to predict the likelihood of avoidable admissions. However, our traditional healthcare system has little incentive to prevent these situations — hospitals view admissions as revenue, while insurance companies have already priced those risks into their premiums. The missing alignment within healthcare meant that many healthcare AI predictions were not helpful. This is where self-insured companies come in. A self-insured company pays the actual care its employees receive. The self-insured industry will grow by 25% in the next seven years. Since the self-insured company bears the actual cost of their employees' healthcare, they have a vested interest in promoting healthier lifestyles. This alignment between the payer and the patient creates a unique dynamic, fostering a shared commitment to health.

Trend 2: The Power of Foundational Models and Predictions. AI's predictive capabilities are no secret. At Marpai, we've harnessed Deep Learning models to forecast high-cost claims events. Most of those predictions offer only a limited time horizon — predicting events within the next 3-6 months. To change disease trajectories we often need to start earlier with preventive care. Enter foundational language models (LLM). LLMs are trained on text predicting the next word. Like the progression of a sentence, diseases often develop in a structured and logical way. Training LLMs on a series of claim codes — the 'words' of our healthcare system — we can predict health events with greater accuracy.

Trend 3: The Ascendancy of Value-Based Care. Introduced in 2008, VBC aims to shift the risk from the payer to the provider, incentivizing providers to deliver value (fee for value) rather than just services (fee for service). The VBC industry has been growing constantly. The US expected to have 42% of national health expenditures in some form of risk sharing arrangement by 2030. Looking at Marpai's data, about 6% of our members have Diabetes type II — the cost for our self-insured clients is 35 million USD. Type 2 Diabetes can be reversed and these costs are avoidable. Over the last few years many new value-based clinical programs launched. At Marpai we looked at over 60 different healthcare programs to partner with. AI and Data allow us to match the best intervention to the best use-case to maximize therapeutic success and improve the ROI.

Three Trends of Transformation. There was no better time than today to transform healthcare. Better AI models extend our predictive capabilities further into the future. Self-insured companies' risk profile and patient needs are aligned. VBC providers are actively reducing risk. It's time to combine all of this and find the right provider for the right member.
