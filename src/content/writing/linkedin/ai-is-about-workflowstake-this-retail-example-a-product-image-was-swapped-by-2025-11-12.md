AI is about #workflows.Take this retail example: a product image was swapped by mistake. The product - "bed assist rails" - is meant to prevent falls now looks like it causes them. That's stupid. "
"A simple #ChatGPT check: “quality control for this product image” would’ve caught it instantly. But the new workflow will likely include creating the image and even shaping its storyline. #AI is changing every workflow in every company."
""
"Here the full answer from ChatGPT - it caught WAY MORE then the wrong image. "
""
"1. Spelling Error"
"“Befor” → should be “Before.”"
" This error reduces professionalism and can lead to listing rejection."
"2. Misleading “Before/After” Presentation"
"The “After” photo implies the product caused a fall, not prevented it."
" This reverses the intended message. The sequence or caption logic should be swapped:"
"Correct would be: “Before (falling)” and “After (using bed rail)” — or simply “Without” / “With.”"
"3. Inconsistent Style & Quality"
"The Before/After collage mixes color (left) and black & white (right). This creates emotional dissonance and looks unpolished."
"Image borders and layout are inconsistent; alignment is slightly off."
"4. Typography Issues"
"Font choice and capitalization are inconsistent (“Bed assist rail can effectively protect you…” should use consistent capitalization or sentence case)."
"Too many different font weights and shapes make it look non-compliant with Amazon’s image guidelines (which prefer minimal text on main images)."
"5. Non-compliance with Amazon’s Main Image Rules"
"Amazon requires:"
"White background"
"Only the product (no lifestyle context, no text, no logos)"
"This image combines lifestyle, text, and marketing claims — acceptable as secondary image, but not as main image."
"6. Potentially Problematic Claim"
"“Can effectively protect you and your family…” could be considered a medical or safety claim. Amazon typically requires substantiation or FDA approval for such wording. Better phrasing: “Helps prevent accidental falls while sleeping.”"
"7. Low Image Resolution / Compression"
"The product photo at the bottom looks slightly compressed with jagged edges around the rail, suggesting low-resolution export (likely <1000 px on the long edge)."
" Amazon recommends at least 1600 px for zoom capability."
