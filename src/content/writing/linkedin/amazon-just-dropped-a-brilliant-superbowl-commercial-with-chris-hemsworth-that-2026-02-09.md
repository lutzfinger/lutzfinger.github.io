#Amazon just dropped a brilliant #SuperBowl commercial with Chris Hemsworth that plays straight into our collective #AI anxiety. In the ad, Chris becomes convinced that the new Alexa+ is basically #HAL from 2001: A Space Odyssey. Alexa closes the pool cover because it’s raining (clearly trying to suffocate him). Chris is tense convinced an evil AI has entered his home."
""
"But as soon as Alexa suggests ordering him a massage Chris tone flips. Anxiety dissolves. Resistance disappears. This is so true. Not only for Alexa which I have not yet tested (my Hello-Google is still bad) but for all AI tools. "
""
"We adopt technology despite our fears. Often because the convenience is irresistible. We’ve seen this pattern before. Germany, for example, was among the loudest critics of Facebook’s data collection practices… and still became one of its strongest markets."
""
"That said, this doesn’t mean the concerns are invalid or that we should stop asking hard questions. We do need to take agency conflicts seriously. We should be explicit about the ambitions and incentives of Amazon, Google, Meta, X and OpenAI. LLMs are not transparent systems, and pretending otherwise would be naïve."
""
"But what we need is serious analysis, governance, and transparency, not fear-mongering. History shows that panic doesn’t slow adoption; it just weakens the quality of the debate. #Convenience will win. The real question is whether we can pair it with informed #oversight and #accountability."
