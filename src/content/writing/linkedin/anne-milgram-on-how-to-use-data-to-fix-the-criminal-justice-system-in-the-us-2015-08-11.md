Anne Milgram on how to use data to fix the criminal justice system in the US. Essentially she proposes a scoring mechanism who is dangerous, who might be violent, who should be ‘safe to be released’. As a data-person I know that the biggest threat in any model is the overconfidence of the user - what if judges relying too much on the algorithm. Scary thought - right? But still the use-case makes a lot of sense to me.
