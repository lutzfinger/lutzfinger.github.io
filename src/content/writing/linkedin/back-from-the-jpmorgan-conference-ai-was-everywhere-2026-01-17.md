Back from the #JPMorgan conference: #AI was everywhere. But let's separate Hype vs Reality. #Reality: LLMs/transformers are powerful because they are great at sequence modeling (notes, longitudinal records, time-ordered events), which translates into better search, better summaries, better patient identification for trials, and less administrative friction. That will make clinical trials faster and healthcare delivery more efficient. #Hype: “AI will design our drugs and replace researchers”. Next-token prediction does not equal conceptual or causal understanding, and in regulated domains “almost right” isn’t good enough without guardrails and real-world validation. The near-term win is that AI speeds up iteration and decision-making with better priors and better filtering, while #humans remain highly needed. "
