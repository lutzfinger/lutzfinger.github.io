Be aware of the "Bitter Lesson"! It highlights a human shortcoming. In all our prompt and guard rail designs we yet again, rely on human domain knowledge but this again is ultimately a constraint. Emaad Manzoor from Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management explained this very nice in this video. "
""
"Historically, we've embedded rules of grammar in NLP or differential equations in computer vision. While effective initially, these human-designed constraints limit deeper learning. As computation and data scale, AI systems reveal that removing these constraints and learning purely from patterns yields superior long-term progress."
""
"It's a 'bitter' lesson because we 'implementing' our machine learning solutions, only to find that raw computation and data offer a more powerful, albeit less intuitive, path forward. "
