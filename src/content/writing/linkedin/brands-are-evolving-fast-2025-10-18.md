#Brands are evolving fast. In the future, a brand won’t just sell products — it will guide creation within its own brand guidelines. When the first AI-generated songs appeared, #Drake pushed back. He should’ve leaned in and allowed others to create under his name. With #SORA2 from OpenAI, we’re getting a glimpse of how this new brand model could work — Sam #Altman lets his own “liking” shape content under SORA’s AI direction. Here’s an example: an AI-generated video of Sam and me announcing that XGEN AI acquired my company, r2decide.
