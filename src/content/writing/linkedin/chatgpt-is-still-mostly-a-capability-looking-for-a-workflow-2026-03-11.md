#ChatGPT is still mostly a capability looking for a workflow. The issue is not technical excellence alone. The problem is packaging. Take #OpenClaw: An Imperfect product, plenty of flaws ... but one thing it got right was the interface. You could chat through familiar interfaces like WhatsApp and Telegram. That mattered more for many than #security. It reduced friction. LLMs entered the workflow."
""
"Most AI products are still a standalone chat windows, disconnected from the real flow of work. Users copy, paste, switch tabs, rewrite prompts, and manually move output from one system to another. "
""
"The winners in AI will be the ones embedded directly into decisions, transactions, communication, and execution. AI only becomes valuable when it disappears into the workflow. And no... I don't mean a chatbot you have to summon. "
""
"The interface is the product and the workflow is the moat."
