Dear Google - You were the first with #LLMs but you did not manage to bring LLMs to the general public. But why - after all this time - can’t we use Google Gemini into your home speakers?  OpenAI announced today that ChatGPT is now accessible on everything—including WhatsApp and even rotary phones? (https://lnkd.in/dWQ4pz8N)"
""
"A few thoughts:"
"1) OpenAI is laser-focused on growing its consumer base to outpace competitors like Google. Making ChatGPT available everywhere—no matter how basic the platform—fits perfectly into that strategy."
"2) Jasper Masemann and I said early on in our podcast (https://lnkd.in/d_8b-FgU) - LLMs are a new interface. Thus also here no surprises that OpenAI tries to establish this interface everywhere."
""
"#Product-wise, this day 11th launch isn’t particularly impressive. The integration (at least for now) doesn’t allow users to log in with their account details, which is a big downside. The #future of chat will be personal, and for that to work, OpenAI needs to know who you are.
