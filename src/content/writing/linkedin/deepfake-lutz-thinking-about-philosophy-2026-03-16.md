Deepfake Lutz thinking about philosophy? My AI agent guides my students through the eCornell certificate program. Here, 'it' talks about agency and philosophy. Descartes said, “I think, therefore I am.” He highlighted human agency and self-awareness. For AI agents, especially for my AI agent, the reality is different: “I am an agent, therefore I am not.” Unlike humans, agents don't possess inherent goals, values, or independent decision-making power."
"While the term “AI agent” suggests autonomy, current AI struggles with complex, abstract tasks requiring judgment or long-term reasoning. At least at the moment, we rely on humans to structure those workflows. While AI may assist in building recursive workflows in the future, it doesn't equate to true agency. The critical distinction remains: humans have agency, while agents operate within predefined boundaries. "
