Enterprise #AI is not about getting a bot to work once. It is about designing workflows your team, your customers, and your auditors can actually trust. In my recent eCornell workshop, we focused on the difference between a flashy prototype and an enterprise-ready agent: atomic steps, clear inputs and outputs, human approval gates, least-privileged tool access, observability, and evals. We even looked at a glassbox demo of how AI “thinks.” TLDR: it does not think. It follows a strict rule system (even a constitution). Intelligence without workflow discipline is just #risk."
"That is where #enterprise thinking begins. Real value does not come from asking AI to “do everything.” It comes from breaking work into controllable tasks, deciding where humans stay in the loop, and building systems that can be monitored, corrected, and improved over time. The winners in AI will not be the ones with the flashiest demos. They will be the ones who turn messy work into reliable workflows. "
