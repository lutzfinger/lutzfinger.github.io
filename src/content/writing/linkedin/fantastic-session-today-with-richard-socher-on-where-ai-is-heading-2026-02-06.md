#Fantastic session today with Richard Socher on where #AI is heading. If you missed it... here are the highlights:"
""
"New job: “#reward engineer” - Not just writing prompts, but telling AI what “good” looks like. Lots of talk about the alignment problem – and how the “human in the loop” becomes central."
""
"We all become AI managers - The new core skill: delegating clearly to AI, adding constraints, and checking results. An #agentic world with humans as managers of AI systems."
""
"Search & discovery stay critical - Richard explained how You.com is becoming core infrastructure for search in the LLM era, keeping models up to date and grounded."
""
"#GEO: marketing to machines - How the marketing game shifts when the “buyer” is an AI. Great insights on how brands can show up in AI answers, not just in classic SEO."
""
"Next frontier: AI #improving AI - In domains where results can be simulated or verified (code, math, some science), AI will increasingly help design, test, and improve new models – pushing beyond human level."
""
"Huge thanks again to Richard, Julia La Roche, Christopher Wofford, David Keslick, Ben Wendel, Chris Tracy and all the other great minds eCornell and the Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management ... and a big thanks to the engaged audience... "
""
