GEO (Generative Engine Optimization). Here the top insights from my discussion with Alex Dees founder at Meridian. TLDR: Customers are increasingly researching, comparing, shortlisting, and deciding before they ever reach your homepage. That means your website is no longer the front door. The model is."
""
"That changes marketing completely."
"• #SEO was about being found. #GEO is about being understood, trusted, and recommended by machines."
"• Traffic may go down, but #intent goes up. The visitor who arrives from AI often comes ready to act."
"• The #website still matters deeply, but now as a machine-readable source of truth."
"• Owned content becomes even more valuable because it compounds."
"• The winners will not just “buy ads” or “buy mentions.” They will build agentic workflows that continuously improve how AI systems understand their brand."
""
"The big shift is this: we are moving from clicks to citations. That is not a small optimization in digital marketing. It is a rewrite of how discovery works."
