Great article from the Cosmos Institute: Should we hand over our decision-making authority to an LLM? The pros are compelling — #AI can reduce human bias, reason at a global scale, coordinate collective action, and optimize personal outcomes with consistent, impartial logic."
" But ceding personal decision power risks eroding autonomy, self-authorship, and the growth that comes from trial and error. AI overlooks unmeasurable values and can lead to alienation and homogenized thinking — after all, #LLMs are just averages."
"As many of you know, I don’t believe #AGI is close, and #GPT5 is not proof of it. AI is a tool. We humans have a unique form of agency — one AI agents simply don’t possess."
