How to change #healthcare? Make VBC accessible to everyone! More below or in the FORBES article:"
""
"Value-Based Care (VBC) is a model that the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) have used with considerable success since its introduction in 2008. It lowers costs and increases health outcomes. As a result, many VBC companies entered the market. "
""
"(1) 40-50% of the commercial self-insured market (around 120 billion USD) is without access to VBC services."
""
"(2) Each #VBC company approaches health management differently, much like the numerous diet plans available in thousands of books. How can a patient know what approach works for them personally? "
""
