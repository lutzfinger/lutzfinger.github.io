How will an #LLM learn about your #brand. Anything that is not "expected" or "average". Let's take #Coffee... “chocolate and nutty taste” is normal. “Peach coffee” is novel and strange."
""
"That is exactly why AI may pick it up. LLMs do not understand taste the way humans do. But they do detect what is unusual relative to the pattern they have seen before. So if your website says what everyone else says, you may be forgettable to AI. If you describe what is truly distinct, AI is more likely to retain that information."
""
"That is a GEO lesson for marketers: "
"Don’t just describe your product. Describe what makes you special. "
