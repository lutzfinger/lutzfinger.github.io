#Humans do not make systems work only by following rules. Often, they make them work by knowing when not to. In my conversation with Emaad Manzoor, we discussed rerouting bags at an airport belt. The system works because humans use judgment, adapt, and sometimes break process to keep things moving."
"That is the challenge with #AI agents. They follow every rule exactly, without sometimes limited context, they may be compliant but still wrong. In complex systems, rigid obedience can create bad outcomes."
""
"The real question is not whether AI can follow rules. It is whether it can handle the moments when the context is not sufficient or when the rules should not be followed."
