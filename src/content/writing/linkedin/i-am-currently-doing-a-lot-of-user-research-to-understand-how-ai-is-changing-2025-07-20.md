I am currently doing a lot of #user #research to understand how #AI is changing #retail. Yesterday a shopper told me during an interview: “I uploaded my blood test to ChatGPT. It suggested vitamins I should get at Sunday.de. I went and ordered €200 worth of vitamines.” This shopper had NEVER (!!!) been to Sunday.de. It just heard about it from ChatGPT. AI recommendations are currently more worth than social endorsements. Retail is being restructured by AI assistants, and brands need to rethink how they get discovered. It's not about SEO anymore—it's about LLM optimization (#LLMO or #GEO)."
