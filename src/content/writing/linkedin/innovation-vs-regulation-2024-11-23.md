#INNOVATION vs #REGULATION! Thank you, Volker Wissing Germany's Minister for Digital, for hosting an inspiring discussion on “Balancing Regulation and Innovation” with Pablo Chavez, Renate Nikolay from the European Commission, Caroline K., Vivek Mahajan, and me."
""
"The #EU is falling behind in the #AI race, but as Pablo Chavez rightly pointed out, #digital #solidarity can foster and scale innovation by creating shared principles. The critical question is: how? Here are my three key takeaways from the discussion:"
""
"(1) "Implementation matters," said Renate Nikolay. She thinks that the "judge is still out" on the impact of the EU AI Act. I agree! In my view, the EU may have moved too quickly, creating a regulatory burden stifling innovation. (Even Giants like Apple hesitate now to launch AI products directly in the EU.)"
"(2) "We can not regulate technology itself," said Vivek Mahajan. Spot on! Take the discussion around AI explainability —great in theory, but far from technical reality. "
"(3) Caroline King emphasized that access to data and expertise is essential for innovation. Absolutely! As I always say, "the ones with data will win"—and companies like SAP can be a great partner for innovation. "
""
"The overarching theme? #Balance! As Emmanuel Macron put it: let’s not “regulate ourselves out of the market.” (https://lnkd.in/dJJB5WtK)
