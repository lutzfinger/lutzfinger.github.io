Created on 2025-04-30 00:58

Published on 2025-04-30 01:08

R2Decide is looking for you - the MBA with the marketing skills that wants to dip their toes into the world of AI and e-commerce. Join us this summer as a Growth Marketing Hacker. This is not your average internship. You’ll be driving the heartbeat of our go-to-market strategy — and in return, you’ll get hands-on training from industry leaders and the chance to build real traction for a real company.
Your Mission (Should You Choose to Accept It):
You’ll lead initiatives across the full growth stack:

Ad placement: From channels to creatives — test what works.

Ad optimization: Build conversion loops. Refine. Scale.

Outreach automation: Email, DM, and partner workflows.

Thought leadership: Help draft a whitepaper that makes people take notice.

Social media stunts: Bring your weirdest and boldest ideas. Let’s break through the noise.

Events / Conferences: Let’s walk the floor to impress the biggest companies
Why This Matters:
This is the kind of internship that builds a growth CMO in 10 weeks. You’ll own KPIs (like signups), manage a budget, and may get engineering support for bigger swings. If you hit your performance targets, we’ll grant equity — because we believe in upside.
Public by Design: We plan to talk openly about what we do, what works, and what fails. Think of this as a growth case study — with your name on it.
About You:
Well... you tell us. But ideally:

You're not an AI. (Sorry, we are discriminating against agents.)

Actually — even better — you aim to beat any AI. We’re looking for execution, judgment, and love. (Good luck finding that in any model.)

You’ve touched the wild world of online marketing — maybe you ran a LinkedIn post that actually worked, launched some ads, or pulled off a growth stunt just for fun.

You’re a communicator — talk, write, meme, pitch. We want personality. And yes, you can use ChatGPT to help. We do.

You’re a little bold, a little experimental, maybe slightly unhinged — in the best way.

You believe in fun, results, and doing epic sh*t (because we do).

Oh yes, did we tell you that you need to love coffee? (just kidding)
How to Apply:
Send your application to jobs@r2decide.com — but don’t just send a resume. We want to see your art.
What’s your best growth hack? A campaign you ran? A viral post? A clever funnel? Surprise us. Impress us. Show us you’ve got the spark.
Others:

Remote-first (EST to PST -timezone) internship

No base salary, but performance-based equity
