Is your brand's #logo looking a little spooked after Halloween? Maybe this was due to the new Logo Transformer from one of my Cornell Tech teams. With just one click, your logo can now dress up for the season's hottest trends. What's next? Maybe logos wearing a turkey disguise for Thanksgiving or donning a Santa hat for the holidays. Congrats to Bharath Swamy, Evan Roberts, Gerhard Nordemann, Mohamed Mohamud Barrow and Jason Feibelman for this fun #AI application. The team trained their own #stablediffusion  LoRA, crafted a Streamlit web app, and adeptly deployed it using AWS SageMaker. Great job on pushing the boundaries of #genAI.
