Just got of an amazing keynote with Lila Tretikov (Partner & Head of AI Strategy at NEA; ex-CEO Wikimedia; ex-Deputy CTO Microsoft), we covered quite some ground."
""
"TLDR: We’re in a new cycle where disruption happens inside the same wave. Companies can hit $10M ARR faster than ever—yet also get disrupted faster than ever. The only sustainable posture is: learn continuously, ship faster, and build defensibility where the model can’t “just eat it.”"
"If you’re building products right now: design for verifiability, memory, and workflow IP—not just demos."
""
"A few topics that really hit home:"
"1) From human feedback to “verifiable reward” training"
"We moved from humans grading outputs (classic RLHF) toward training setups where you can define a correct end state and let the model iterate its way there—like a gym environment (the “AlphaZero-style” intuition). When a problem has a clear “true north,” accuracy improves fast."
""
"2) The next frontier isn’t accuracy — it’s memory + open-ended discovery"
"Lila’s framework:"
"Accuracy: increasingly solvable when answers are verifiable."
"Memory / continuous learning: still messy (short vs long-term, context windows, external stores)."
"Open-endedness: the real AGI-adjacent challenge—problems where we don’t know the answer (biology, chemistry, complex research). This is where models must discover what the next step even is."
""
"3) “AI = quality + control”"
"Speed is exploding, but reliability, guardrails, and verifiability become the differentiator—especially for agentic workflows. Easy setup is not the same as safe operation."
""
"4) SaaS isn’t dead — but the moat is moving"
"If SaaS is “UI + database access,” AI eats that interface layer. Workflows (and domain specificity) matter more, but even those will face pressure as models get better. The durable advantage shifts to data, distribution, and defensible workflow IP."
""
"5) Product management is becoming more technical (again)"
"We may be heading toward a world where it’s not “1 PM : 10 engineers,” but closer to the reverse. PMs who can specify systems precisely, design workflows, and build eval + test harnesses will win. Test-driven development and AI CI aren’t “nice-to-haves”—they become mandatory."
""
"6) VC is already running on AI (but humans still matter)"
"VCs use AI for sourcing, diligence, and signal detection—pulling from everything from public footprints to developer signals. But Lila emphasized a critical point: don’t outsource relationships to agents. Trust, conviction, and partnership are still human."
