Key discussion around AI and value: Stefano Puntoni highlighted the work from Hamsa Bastani that fits LLM capability to an S-curve. In his post he says: “the Silicon Valley narrative of continued exponential growth may be a delusion.”  It's a good reminder that we should separate model progress from value creation."
""
"We’re entering a decade of AI agents. The next wave is less about headline capability leaps and more about adoption and integration: it's about AI tools + permissions + workflow + evals reliability (quote from Jan Oberhauser). Those lead to cost/performance and change management and create compounded value. Most likely expoential for a while."
""
"If models commoditize, advantage shifts up the stack to whoever owns distribution, workflow surfaces, and the data/integration footprint. That’s why so much excitement is around companies like Sierra, n8n, Abridge, Lovable, Rippling, Replit, Runway and many others... It's not about the model per se but as well about the applications. And yes! At the same time, people like Richard Socher and Yann LeCun will keep hunting for the next model generation whether it's recursive training or world models. Once they happen they could again reset the business dynamics. "
