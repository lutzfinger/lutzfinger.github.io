Marietje Schaake shared her thoughts on #Europe. I disagree: The #US isn’t disengaging from Europe—at least not when it comes to AI. The real issue is that it doesn’t want to be "regulated" by Europe. The U.S. aims to maintain its global dominance #AI, which is a pillar of economic and geopolitical power. If AI creates the fourth industrial revolution, securing influence is a strategic necessity. The #EU, had prioritized citizen protection as their focus of the regulation. Don't get me wrong — an admirable goal but one that inevitably slows innovation. That’s why the U.S. is pushing back. "
