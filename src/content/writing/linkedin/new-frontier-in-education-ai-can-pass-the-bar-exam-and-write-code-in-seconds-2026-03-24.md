New Frontier in Education: #AI can pass the Bar exam and write code in seconds. So what, exactly, is #education still for? "
""
"Educators are no longer needed to aggregate information. #Google and #OpenAI already do that faster and, in many cases, better. We are also not needed to test whether student's understanding. With AI, most tests can be passed easily. I have already “replaced” myself two years ago in my online certificate with an AI bot, because educators are no longer needed for those tasks. "
""
"The #new job of education is to teach people how to work effectively with AI: how to question it, challenge it, verify it, and know when not to trust it. AI is powerful, but it works on patterns and averages. It can be brilliant. It can also be wrong with great confidence."
""
"Check out Steve Shaw and Gideon Nave paper... They argues that when people use AI, they often slip into “cognitive surrender”, accepting machine output with little scrutiny. When the AI was right, performance improved. When it was wrong, people still followed it too easily. "
""
"That is the new educational frontier. Education’s new north star is teaching judgment to evaluate the answer a machine gives you."
