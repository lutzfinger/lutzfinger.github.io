No, #AGI is not near. How do we know? Because we have more and more specialized models to choose from. The "G" in AGI stands for "general"—meaning an AI that understands what you need without being explicitly directed. In contrast, let's look at OpenAI today. There are more narrow AI functions than ever. AI is a tool: we use a chess AI for chess, a medical LLM for medical questions, and so on. We still have to choose the right model for each task. What we’re witnessing is a rise in highly capable AI systems excelling at specific tasks. Exciting? Absolutely. General? Not yet!"
