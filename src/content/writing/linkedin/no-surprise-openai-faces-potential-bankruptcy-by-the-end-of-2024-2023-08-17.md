No surprise… #openai faces potential bankruptcy by the end of 2024. If you've been following my content or are one of my Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management students, you'll know my reservations about investing in OpenAI. Here's a quick breakdown:"
""
"Constructing Large Language Models (#LLMs) like #gpt comes with a hefty price tag. For an insightful overview, take a look at Guido Appenzeller 's analysis (see comments). Is this price-tag worth it? Only if LLMs would work like a foundry business for chips - meaning only if this investment creates a business moat. TL;DR: it doesn't. "
""
"(1) ALGORITHM != PRODUCT"
"Fundamentally, an LLM is just a #machinelearning  #algorithm designed for sequence-to-sequence predictions. An algorithm is not inherently a product and may or may not create enterprise value. This has been true in the early 2000's as the Data Science hype started. (see the link to my book about data & value). It's still holds true today. It's worth noting that ChatGPT is a product derived from OpenAI's LLMs. But ChatGPTs success is based on more than the LLM: It’s UX, it’s access, it’s convenience, it’s plugins etc."
""
"(2) ALGORITHM != MOAT"
"Historically, algorithms haven't been significant barriers to entry. At best it was a small time advantage. This held true with LLMs. Anyone can train a transformer; even if those might have fewer tokens or parameters, they still might be adequate for a specific product. Most of the startups I advise shifted away from OpenAI in favor of more accessible open-source alternatives because it’s sufficient. "
""
"(3) MOAT == Data || UX"
"From what I've observed, the real competitive edge for many products that use LLMs lies either in their user interface – how they integrate into workflows (see my comments about why Jasper.ai was doomed) – or in accessing exclusive data (see podcast about private vs. public data)."
