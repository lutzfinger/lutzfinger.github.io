One big problem with Large Language Models (LLMs)? They don’t know how to say, "I don’t know." Instead, they guess the "next best word" based on patterns, not actual knowledge. This is a built-in flaw in the technology."
"One way to fix this? Connect LLMs to real data using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)—basically, adding a database to the model. Google's Data Commons team from R. Guha and Prem Ramaswami does exactly this:  helping models like GEMINI think better using organized facts."

"Maybe (!) we don’t need Zuck at Meta to fact-check? Instead, we could let #AI handle reviews of correlations and do feasibility checks? "
