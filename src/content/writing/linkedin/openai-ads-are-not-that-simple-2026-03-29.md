OpenAI #ads are not that simple. Google’s model worked because incentives aligned: users clicked, left, and advertisers got traffic. ChatGPT is different. It is built for retention, not redirection."
"So the big question is simple: why should brands pay for ads inside a system that does not really want the user to leave? That is why AI advertising is still unresolved. The issue is not format. It is incentive design."
