Our future with #LLMs explained: I asked #Meta about a medication for a dog. Meta refused. Why? Meta: “There’s not much of a market for it.” What does this mean for us? "
""
"(a) Product Manager insight:"
"I assume Meta has guardrails that prohibit discussions about health. That’s a liability concern. But the implementation here is wrong. This is a product manager failure. The future role of product managers is to think about evaluations and to design workflows that respond in a helpful way, even when the answer itself is restricted."
""
"(b) Consumer insight:"
"The lens “Is this monetizable?” is real. LLMs cost money. All providers, including Meta, need to figure out how to make money. LLMs will increasingly try to sell you things. No surprise brand are scrambling to crack GEO / AEO / LLMO—essentially ensuring that the AI talks about their brand. As a consumer, you need to understand this new reality."
""
"(c) Society insight:"
"There’s an uncomfortable question we need to face: Who decides what the AI stays silent about? What knowledge fades because it doesn’t convert? What questions quietly die because they’re “bad for business”? This isn’t sci-fi. If LLMs are becoming the default interface to reality—who controls them?"
