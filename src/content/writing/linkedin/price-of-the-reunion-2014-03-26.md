Price of the Reunion"
""
"Having friends like YOU is priceless... but when it comes the the re-union there is a PRICE. Some of us might feel the price is too high. Thus we asked INSEAD for a statement on the price. Here is their feedback: INSEAD did a survey to know that we (the alumni) want to party in the chateau instead of the cheaper 'tent' option. INSEAD is heavily subsidizing our tickets so that we can come back and meet our friends."
""
"THE ORIGINAL EMAIL RESPONDS:"
""About the Ball cost, we would really like to be transparent with you all on these details and this is why we have chosen to do a more detailed pre-reunion survey this year with a certain notion of pricing based on previous years’ experience. "
"The chateau de Fontainebleau is particularly challenging for us in that it is the most splendid venue for the summer ball with great feedback after the event but this comes at a cost due to renting, insurance (amongst many) - it is a protected historical monument in France."
"In terms of overall pricing, INSEAD also heavily subsidizes the reunions to bring the pricing down - giving you the possibility to engage with your class mates and high attendance of the reunion are both very important factors to us."
