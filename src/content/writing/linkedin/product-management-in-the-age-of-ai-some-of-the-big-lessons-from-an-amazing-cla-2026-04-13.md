Product Management in the Age of AI: Some of the big lessons from an amazing class at Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management. Most #product managers were trained for deterministic software. AI changes the job. The #AI PM is different in at least five crucial ways:"
""
"1) User-centricity matters more than ever"
"As coding gets easier to outsource to tools, the scarce skill is no longer building faster. It is choosing the right problem and understanding the user’s workflow deeply."
""
"2) Workflow completion beats feature novelty"
"Users do not want a random AI chatbot dropped into their app. They want to get important work done faster, cheaper, and with less cognitive load."
""
"3) You have to design for probability"
"Traditional software is deterministic. AI is probabilistic. That means PMs must think in terms of confidence, fallbacks, guardrails, escalation paths, and human review."
""
"4) Trust is a core product feature"
"Accuracy alone is not enough. Users need to understand what the system did, why it did it, and when they should not trust it."
""
"5) Ops and evals never stop"
"The job does not end at launch. AI products require continuous monitoring for prompt drift, model changes, latency, cost, quality, and safety."
