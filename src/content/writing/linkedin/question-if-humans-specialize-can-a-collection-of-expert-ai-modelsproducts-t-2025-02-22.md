QUESTION: "If humans specialize, can a collection of expert AI models/products together be called #AGI?" - Aneesh Chopra the first-ever CTO of the White House and a member of the National #AI Advisory Committee and I got asked this question during our recent LIVE show."
""
"ANSWER: Yes, AI is evolving toward specialized models—known as narrow AI. I wrote in Forbes, "No AGI, But a Killer App" (https://lnkd.in/dZZbcHNX) , that many smaller AIs together will excel in areas like law, science, and conversation. This #multiagent approach offers key benefits such as scalability & efficiency. But it also allows us to easier fine-tune and govern AI. I explored this in "Is AI Replacing Us?" (https://lnkd.in/dCSM4x8A) — rather than replacing jobs, multi-agent AI enhances productivity, making humans strategists and decision-makers, not obsolete workers."
