""
"ANSWER: There is loads ongoing. One notable project is TC Labs, which recently earned a sustainability award from INSEAD. It's founded and led by Paul Manwell. The company is developing AI-driven tools to help heavy industry businesses improve their operations. His work is dedicated to cutting energy costs and lowering carbon emissions, fostering sustainability in industries like steel, cement, refining & chemicals. By utilizing advanced analytics and artificial intelligence, TC Labs seeks to convert large volumes of unstructured data into meaningful insights, optimizing industrial processes and enhancing overall efficiency."
