Reimagining healthcare with value-based care! At Marpai, Inc., we've found that a scary 41% of our healthcare cost comes from managing chronic diseases that could benefit from #valuebasedcare. However, getting access to exceptional providers like Virta Health, Vori Health and many others can be challenging for the self-insured."
""
"That's why today, we're thrilled to introduce Marpai Connect - a groundbreaking platform leveraging #AI and #data to seamlessly connect our members with the right healthcare providers. Our aim? To improve health for our members and alleviate the impact of chronic diseases on our clients."
""
"The future of healthcare is not just on the horizon, it's here! "
