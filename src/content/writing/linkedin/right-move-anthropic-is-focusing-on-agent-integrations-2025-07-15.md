RIGHT MOVE: Anthropic is focusing on agent integrations. I had suspected that this is their strategy for a while now. The question isn’t who has the best model - it’s who has the best model for WHAT VALUE. (yes, good old business value still matters). We’re far from #AGI (just count the many different models that OpenAI has by now - one for every need). But if AI is just a tool, then the real value lies in workflow integration. WHERE DO WE GO FROM HERE? OpenAI seems set on owning the entire stack. They want to extend vertically into every industry. Anthropic, in contrast, is positioning itself as the toolkit others can build on - horizontal integration. Smart strategy.
