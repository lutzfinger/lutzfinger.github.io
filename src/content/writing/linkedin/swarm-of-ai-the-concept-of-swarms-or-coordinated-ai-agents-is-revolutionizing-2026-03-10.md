Swarm of AI: The concept of 'swarms' or coordinated AI agents is revolutionizing how we approach complex tasks. Instead of manually prompting an AI for multiple steps (ideation, writing, editing, marketing), a swarm allows distinct #agents, each with a specific skill (like a writer or editor), to work in tandem. A coordination skill defines their communication and workflow protocols."
""
"This collaborative approach creates powerful feedback loops, accelerating processes and enabling more sophisticated outputs. It shifts the paradigm from single-prompt interaction to orchestrating a team of specialized AI agents. "
