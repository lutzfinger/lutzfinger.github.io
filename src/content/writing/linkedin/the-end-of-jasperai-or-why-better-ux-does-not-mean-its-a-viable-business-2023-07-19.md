The End of Jasper.AI or why better UX does not mean it's a Viable Business: "
""
"Large Language Models (#LLMs) are revolutionizing our interactions with machines. But essentially, they are a new interface. Similar to Microsoft Windows or the Apple iPhone. Also, those were game-changers. "
""
"NOTE tough: a new interface alone doesn't create a new business or product: Google just launched an LLM on top of Google Docs, a seamless integration that significantly enhances the user experience. That's the end of Jasper.AI."
""
"This is only one example. We can expect to see a wave of dead-in-the-water startups. How about document search services using LLMs? MS will win. Or LLMs accessing your database? Amazon will win. "
""
"New business opportunities are here... but it's more complex than just adding an LLM to something that others do. #TechInnovation
