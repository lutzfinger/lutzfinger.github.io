"The future is Indie" that was one of the main conclusions Jasper Masemann and I had early on. Generative AI drives down cost of content producers. Check out Amanda Jang - one of my amazing students at Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management - on the side she creates mini-videos: ChatGPT for ideas - AIPRM as marketplace for prompts -"
"Leonardo.Ai and Midjourney for text-to-image generation - ElevenLabs for converting text to speech - and CapCut for crafting engaging videos. 30min later you have a nice TikTok Story. WELL DONE! As Guido Appenzeller highlighted in his talk "film is next"."
""
"WARNING: Before you dive into starting up the next new thing... AI is more of an extension of business. For instance, jasper.ai was impressive until Google incorporated similar features into Google Docs. Tome.ai stunned us until Microsoft introduced Co-Pilot in its presentation software. Thus wait for it... soon storyboarding and scriptwriting software like FinalDraft and Backlight will use AI and leverage their market access. "
""
"In the meantime, enjoy Amanda's videos... they are fun.
