The future of SAAS - Explained via Quickbooks: A lot of SaaS companies are about to learn that their product advantage was never the workflow. It was just the interface. And AI is taking that away."
"#QuickBooks is a terrible interface. It's not simple. It's not helpful. But once Gemini as a plug in, can watch the screen, understand what is happening, it can guide me, the user, step by step. Quickbooks bad UX stops being the main problem.  "
"For the last 20 years, SaaS competed on cleaner screens, smarter navigation, and better user flows. Now the software does not need to be easy for me. It needs to be legible to AI."
"In that world, “beautiful SaaS” is less valuable than “AI-operable SaaS.”"
"That is why AI will reshape software faster than most product teams expect."
