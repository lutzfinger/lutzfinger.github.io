The International AI Safety Report, co-authored by Yoshua Bengio, provides a concise yet thorough analysis of AI risks. It emphasizes that AI doesn’t evolve in isolation—human choices will determine whether it becomes a force for good or harm. Key risks and recommendations are outlined below. That said, given the current political climate, I doubt these recommendations will gain much traction."

"C. Systemic Risks - such as Labour Market Disruptions - and Market Concentration"
