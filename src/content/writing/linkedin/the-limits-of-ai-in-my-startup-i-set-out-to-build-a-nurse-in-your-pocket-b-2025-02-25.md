The Limits of AI: In my startup - I set out to build a "#nurse in your pocket" but ended up in #eCommerce. Why? Because healthcare demands real compassion, and AI struggles empathy. Listen in to my UX story about #compassion - At the end I have built R2Decide—a platform designed for ecommerce that leverages generative AI to create dynamic content elements based on product data and user-generated input. These content elements seamlessly integrate across the entire storefront—from search to product pages—enhancing the user experience without disruption. But don't give up on healthcare...
