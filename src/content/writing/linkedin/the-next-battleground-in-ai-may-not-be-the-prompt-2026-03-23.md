The next battleground in AI may not be the prompt. It may be memory."
"Prompt injection is often discussed as a one-time attack inside a conversation. But the bigger risk is persistent memory. Once information gets stored there, it becomes future context. And for an AI agent, context is effectively truth."
"That is why GEO is getting more interesting — and more dangerous. It is no longer only about influencing what gets surfaced. It is about influencing what gets remembered."
"In an agentic world, memory becomes infrastructure. And whoever shapes that memory shapes behavior."
