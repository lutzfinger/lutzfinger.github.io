The next economy will not be built around humans clicking through apps or webpages. It will be built around agents negotiating with other agents."
"That is the real meaning of the agentic economy. For years, software was a tool. Then AI became an assistant. Now it is starting to become an economic actor."
""
"An agent can already do more than answer questions. It can search, compare, negotiate, bid, and transact on your behalf. And when it hits a gap — missing context, physical action, human judgment — it can escalate that task to a person."
""
"That changes the structure of our economy. We move from:"
"human-to-human trade"
"to"
"agent-to-agent trade"
"and increasingly"
"agent-to-human transactions"
"In that world, the interface matters less than the protocol."
"The brand matters less than the access."
"And value shifts to whoever controls trust, permissions, data, and execution."
""
"The big question is no longer:"
" “What can AI answer?”"
"It is:"
" “What can AI negotiate, buy, sell, and execute?”"
""
