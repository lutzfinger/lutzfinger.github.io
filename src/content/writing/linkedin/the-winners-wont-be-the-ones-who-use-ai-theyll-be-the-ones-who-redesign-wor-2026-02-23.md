The winners won’t be the ones who “use AI.” They’ll be the ones who redesign work around it. AI is an operating model story more than a technology story."
"That was the core leadership lesson from a great session this week in downtown San Francisco with Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management Dean, Vishal Gaur, and a room full of Cornell alumni and EMBAs."
""
"A few hot takes of the evening..."
"⚪  Same headline, opposite strategy: “Layoffs” can signal enablement (forcing workflow automation / tool adoption) orreplacement (a reset after missed cycles). Great leaders don’t copy tactics—they pick the right move for the moment and context."
"⚪  Agency becomes the new moat: AI can draft, summarize, and generate options. Humans still own selection, tradeoffs, and accountability. The best leaders build leverage by enabling teams to become “AI-native,” not by outsourcing judgment."
"⚪  Workflow design beats prompt wizardry: Reliability comes from systems: atomize tasks → constrain outputs → verify → iterate. The biggest performance gains aren’t model upgrades—they’re better workflows."
"⚪  Ethics isn’t a slogan—it’s an operating discipline: Responsible AI requires measurable systems—guardrails, evals, audits, and escalation paths—because you can’t “policy” your way out of thousands of daily AI decisions."
"⚪  LLMs are probabilistic. Healthcare runs on protocols (“if X, then Y”). That mismatch is a mismatch. The winning pattern will be AI wrapped in strict workflows: grounded context, constrained decisions, clear responsibility."
