This is a common #misperception that Jakub Polec cites here. The storyline usually goes like this:"
" (1) #AI is built on averages — TRUE."
" (2) Humanity is now #doomed because we’re learning only from those averages — nonsense. That’s as wrong as past hype that #AGI would imminently replace us. Let’s unpack this:"
" (A) CORRECT: We do see more #SLOB (Synthetic Low-Quality Output of Bots). The barrier to create has dropped dramatically; everyone can publish now."
" (B) CORRECT: Model output shouldn’t be recycled as training #data. Models generate averages, and feeding those averages back in causes model degradation."
" (C) INCORRECT: Not all models will inevitably decline. That’s simply not how modern training pipelines work."
""
"For instance, Elon Musk’s xAI reportedly spends around US $1 billion per month on training. A major portion of that goes into pairwise model comparisons—filtering out low-quality generations. If the model’s outputs degrade (because they’re SLOB), that data is excluded. So, just because there’s more SLOB in the wild doesn’t mean all AI systems will regress toward mediocrity. Far from it."
"And one last point: this isn’t peer-reviewed #Oxford research. Jakub didn’t cite any empirical sources—it comes from Graphite, a company selling AEO/SEO tools. So we can assume a certain self-interest in amplifying the “AI eats its own tail” narrative.
