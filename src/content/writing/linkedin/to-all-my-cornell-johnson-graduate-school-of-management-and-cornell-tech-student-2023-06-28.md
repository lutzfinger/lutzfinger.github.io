To all my Cornell Johnson Graduate School of Management and Cornell Tech students, I've had the privilege of teaching in the past nine years - you are amazing. Our class time together was often enlightening. My teaching journey started in 2014 with a short course on data and products (Thanks to Soumitra Dutta and Vishal Gaur). Next year I will teach five classes on #machinelearning, #data, #products, #analytics, and the influence of #AI on society."
""
"Especially about AI's societal impact, I have learned the most from you all. Your projects about data products and our class dialogues were truly inspirational. In the area of ChatGPT, it has become trendy to talk about AGI and doomsday scenarios. I see much of the large language models as a UX interface. It enhances our way of working with computers. Machine learning and AI will enhance our abilities and turbocharge our potential. We will see many new products, and we, the consumer, will be winning. Personalized access to education, health services, legal support, and much more will be in reach for many. Undoubtedly, these technologies will change how we work and communicate, which won't be easy for everyone. But, having worked with all of you for the past nine years, I am confident you will help create a better world with AI. YOU ROCK!!! I am looking forward to the next term and many more amazing classes."
""
"BTW: For those who miss the classroom discussions and my German accent, you might want to tune into my podcast on “AI" with Jasper Masemann. I'll share the link in the comments below. "
