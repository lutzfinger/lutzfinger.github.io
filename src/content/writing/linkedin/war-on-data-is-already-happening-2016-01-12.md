War on data is already happening. Who is allowed to have data and who can interpret it? Those will be the discussions of our future to keep and protect power. An interesting law in Wyoming makes it a crime to “collect resource data” from any “open land”. According to Justin Pidot, the law was passed to conceal dangerous contamination issues.
