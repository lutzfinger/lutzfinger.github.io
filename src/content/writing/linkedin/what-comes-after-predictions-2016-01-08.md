What comes after predictions? Fake Cats: Machine learning is often used to predict things based on a training set. Typically, the algorithm answers whether something is fraud transaction or whether there is a cat in the picture or not. Beyond predictions are generative algorithms - programs that create new pictures based on a training set. How about Fake Cats… This video shows you how a neural network learns to generate new cats. None of those cats exists at the end… That’s pretty cool!
