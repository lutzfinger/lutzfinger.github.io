Who Will Win in #AI? Who in #Europe? Who in the #US? Did I Miss Anyone?"
"Despite all the buzz around DeepSeek, OpenAI, and others, the true winners in AI will be those with #data and #customers - meaning established corporations. Just like the mobile revolution, where incumbents extended their value creation into mobile, AI will likely follow the same pattern."
""
"So, who are the top 10 AI-focused companies leading this transformation? Here my pick... what do you think?"
""
"UNITED STATES"
"1. OpenAI: Leader in generative AI"
"2. NVIDIA: Dominates AI hardware with GPUs critical for training models. "
"3. Microsoft: Embedding AI into ... "everything" "
"4. Alphabet Inc. (Google): Embedding AI into ... "everything". Developed Gemini models, Waymo and AlphaFold"
"5. META: Open-source AI models like Llama"
"6. IBM: Watson AI for enterprise solutions"
"7. Amazon (AWS). Leading cloud AI infrastructure with services like SageMaker and Bedrock. "
"8. Intel Corporation: the 'wannabe' NVIDIA"
"9. Palantir Technologies: Advanced data analytics - mainly because they have data access."
"10. Databricks: Provides a cloud-based platform for AI development."
""
"EUROPE"
"Since European corporations are lagging, you'll see more "pure-play" AI companies below."
"1. SAP: Integrates AI into ... "everything""
"2. ASML: AI for chip production - essential for AI hardware"
"3. UiPath: Robotic process automation - they did agents before we knew that it's a thing. "
"4. Graphcore: processor specifically designed for AI and machine learning workloads"
"5. Synthesia: ever saw my Fake Lutz? That's them. Specialize in AI-driven video generation"
"6. Mistral AI: open-weight large language models"
"7. Aleph Alpha: large language models"
"8. Stability AI: open-source generative image models"
"9. KONUX: Combines AI and IoT to provide smart monitoring solutions"
"10. Helsing: AI defense company developing software for defense"
