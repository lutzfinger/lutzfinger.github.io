Why you should not trust OpenAI blindly? OpenAI is mostly based on a Large Language Model. It will answer according to the information it has stored in from the trainings data. Essentially picture it as a big Database. The issue is, that it is that this database is outdated. OpenAI claims Garmin does not have EKG functionality. Well, check out you.com founded by Richard Socher - also they use LLMs but combined with RAG (retrieval augmented generation). They know that Garmin has a wide range of EKG watches.
